## Synopsis: El fugitivo en el Jura (The Fugitive in the Jura) — Heinrich Zschokke

El fugitivo en el Jura places a fugitive in the Jura, the folded country on the French-Swiss edge, a classic landscape for hiding. Flight and terrain are the title's whole machine.

No author is named, so no historical identity will be assigned to the runner. Mountains that complicate a pursuit are the setting.

It is an adventure of escape, or a moral tale about someone who must not be found. The Jura is the constant.
