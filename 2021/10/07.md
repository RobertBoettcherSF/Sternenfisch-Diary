## Synopsis: En las montañas de la locura: Por H. P. Lovecraft (At the Mountains of Madness) — Howard Phillips Lovecraft

En las montañas de la locura: Por H. P. Lovecraft is Lovecraft's At the Mountains of Madness in Spanish. Scientists in Antarctica find a range and a city that rewrite the history of life, and the narrator writes to keep the next expedition from going.

The horror is archaeological. The prose imitates a report, which makes the unreportable worse.

It is a long tale of discovery as contamination.
