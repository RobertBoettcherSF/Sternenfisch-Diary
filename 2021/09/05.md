**Themes:** Fruit, Image, Title

## Synopsis: リンゴ

リンゴ is the Japanese word for apple, and that word is the whole title. No author is named, and no plot will be built out of a fruit.

An apple in a title can mean a still life, a childhood, a lesson, or a single object looked at until it becomes strange. Only the pages know which.

The honest invitation is the image itself: one ordinary thing, given a cover.
