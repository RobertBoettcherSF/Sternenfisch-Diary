## Synopsis: Orgueil et Préjugés — Jane Austen

Orgueil et Préjugés is Jane Austen's novel of Elizabeth Bennet and Mr. Darcy, in the language of this edition. A family of daughters must marry, a proud man must be reread, and a witty woman must discover that her first accuracy was incomplete.

Money, embarrassment, and a great house do the structural work. The comedy is the correction of a confident mistake.

The story does not depend on the English title to be itself. The characters survive the crossing.
