## Synopsis: Giuseppe Fouché (Joseph Fouché) — Stefan Zweig

Giuseppe Fouché is Zweig's portrait of Joseph Fouché in the language of this edition. The subject is a man who served every France that would have him and outlived the principals.

Police, revolution, empire, and restoration are the weather. The character is the constant: usefulness without devotion.

It is a biography of a secondary figure who keeps turning out to be central.
