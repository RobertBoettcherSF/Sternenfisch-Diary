# 2021-06-28 — 十九歳から四十歳まで

**Date:** 2021-06-28

**Title:** 十九歳から四十歳まで

**Themes:** Life, Years, Becoming

## Synopsis: 十九歳から四十歳まで

十九歳から四十歳まで measures a life from nineteen to forty, the years in which most people become who they are going to be for a long time. No author is named, and no career will be invented to occupy those years.

The form suggested is retrospective: a span, not a single crisis. Youth's last decade and adulthood's first are the subject.

The book is about duration. The title already chooses its window.
