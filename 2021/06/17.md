**Themes:** Animals, Senses, World

## Synopsis: Ambiente e mondo interno degli animali — Jakob von Uexküll

Ambiente e mondo interno degli animali is Uexküll's account of animal worlds, in the language of this edition. Each creature perceives a different set of signs. The environment is not a single stage on which every species is an actor with the same script.

The idea of the Umwelt, a surrounding world of meaning, is the book's gift to later thought.

It changes how a walk looks. The dog, the bird, and the insect are not in your picture of the street. They are in theirs.

## Synopsis: Joseph Fouché: Portrait d'un homme politique — Stefan Zweig

Joseph Fouché: Portrait d'un homme politique is Zweig's portrait of Joseph Fouché in the language of this edition. The subject is a man who served every France that would have him and outlived the principals.

Police, revolution, empire, and restoration are the weather. The character is the constant: usefulness without devotion.

It is a biography of a secondary figure who keeps turning out to be central.
