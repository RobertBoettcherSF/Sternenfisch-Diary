## Synopsis: Milieu en innerlijke wereld van dieren — Jakob von Uexküll

Milieu en innerlijke wereld van dieren is Uexküll's account of animal worlds, in the language of this edition. Each creature perceives a different set of signs. The environment is not a single stage on which every species is an actor with the same script.

The idea of the Umwelt, a surrounding world of meaning, is the book's gift to later thought.

It changes how a walk looks. The dog, the bird, and the insect are not in your picture of the street. They are in theirs.
