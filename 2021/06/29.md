## 1984

**Date:** 2021-06-29

**Title:** 1984

**Author:** George Orwell

**Themes:** Surveillance, Language, Power

## Synopsis: 1984 — George Orwell

1984 is George Orwell's novel of a society ruled by perpetual war, a rewritten past, and a screen that watches back. Winston Smith works at the Ministry of Truth, altering records, and tries to keep a private corner of his mind.

A love affair and a diary become political acts. The state answers with a method that does not merely punish dissent but unmakes it.

The book is a picture of power that wants the inside of a thought, not only obedience in the street.

## Fünfzig Essays

**Date:** 2021-06-29

**Title:** Fünfzig Essays

**Author:** Unknown

**Themes:** Essays, Ideas, Reading

## Synopsis: Fünfzig Essays — Unknown

Fünfzig Essays is a set of fifty essays, a book made for browsing rather than for a single plot. Each piece is a short try at an idea, and the number is the design.

The pleasure of this kind of book is accumulation: one subject, then another, with the freedom to stop. Essays let a writer think in public without pretending to finish the world.

It is a reading book for people who like to carry a few pages and a sentence worth keeping.
