**Author:** Unknown

**Language:** Spanish

**Themes:** Essays, Ideas, Reading

## Synopsis: Cincuenta ensayos (Fifty Essays) — Unknown

Cincuenta ensayos is a set of fifty essays, a book made for browsing rather than for a single plot. Each piece is a short try at an idea, and the number is the design.

The pleasure of this kind of book is accumulation: one subject, then another, with the freedom to stop. Essays let a writer think in public without pretending to finish the world.
