## Synopsis: Peters moon ride — Gerdt von Bassewitz

Peters moon ride is the children's journey to the moon associated with Gerdt von Bassewitz's tale of little Peter. A beetle has lost a leg, and the night's errand to get it back becomes a voyage past the nursery.

The story's charm is domestic magic: a familiar room opening onto a sky with errands, not an epic of conquest. Fear and kindness share the trip.

It has stayed in German childhood as a Christmas dream with a practical mission.
