**Themes:** Smallness, Life, Title

## Synopsis: リトルマン — Hans Fallada

リトルマン says 'little man' in Japanese and stops there. No author is given, so it will not be quietly reassigned to a famous novel that happens to use those words.

The phrase itself is the theme: an ordinary person, scaled without grandeur, perhaps comic and perhaps crushed. The book decides.

What a browser can trust is the scale. This is not a title that pretends to be about kings.
