# 2021-01-11 — The strips of paper and The way through the air

**Date:** 2021-01-11

**Title:** The strips of paper

**Themes:** Paper, Clue, Fragment

## Synopsis: The strips of paper

The strips of paper is strips of paper, scraps that might be letters, evidence, jokes, or a life cut into pieces. No author, no conspiracy constructed here.

The image is fragmentary on purpose. A book with this title asks a reader to care about remnants.

What the strips say is the story. The title only swears that they matter.

**Date:** 2021-01-11

**Title:** The way through the air

**Themes:** Air, Passage, Journey

## Synopsis: The way through the air

The way through the air is a way through the air, a route that does not stay on the road. Flight, spirit, radio, birds, or a mystical path could claim that phrase. No author chooses.

The note keeps the spatial idea and drops the rest. A passage above the ordinary ground is the subject.

It is a title of movement. The vehicle, if there is one, is inside the book.
