## Barthli the Korber

**Date:** 2021-05-22

**Title:** Barthli the Korber

**Author:** Jeremias Gotthelf

**Themes:** Craft, Humility, Basket

## Synopsis: Barthli the Korber — Jeremias Gotthelf

Barthli the Korber is the story of Barthli the basket-maker, a small craftsman whose uprightness is the whole sermon and the whole plot. Gotthelf builds a life out of a trade other people overlook.

Poverty is not prettified. Neither is the quiet authority of someone who does one thing honestly.

It is a short novel of character, Swiss and concrete.

## Hans Berner and his sons

**Date:** 2021-05-22

**Title:** Hans Berner and his sons

**Author:** Jeremias Gotthelf

**Themes:** Family, Sons, Farm

## Synopsis: Hans Berner and his sons — Jeremias Gotthelf

Hans Berner and his sons names Hans Berner and his sons. Without a fuller public plot to lean on, the honest description is a family story: a father, the boys who will inherit or waste what he built, and the tension that structure always holds.

In the Swiss tales this catalog often draws on, that tension is practical, about land and character together.

The pages, not a guessed scandal, are the place to learn how this particular household goes.

## How Anne Bäbi Jowäger keeps house and how he is doing with his doctoring

**Date:** 2021-05-22

**Title:** How Anne Bäbi Jowäger keeps house and how he is doing with his doctoring

**Author:** Jeremias Gotthelf

**Themes:** Medicine, Mother, Village

## Synopsis: How Anne Bäbi Jowäger keeps house and how he is doing with his doctoring — Jeremias Gotthelf

How Anne Bäbi Jowäger keeps house and how he is doing with his doctoring is the story carried in the name Anne Bäbi Jowäger: a village woman confident in her own doctoring, and a household where love and remedy get dangerously mixed. The title's length is the plot's outline.

Part One and Part Two divide the same matter. A son, a mother, and the question of who is allowed to heal are what the name has meant to readers of this Swiss novel.

It is a book about care that does harm when certainty replaces knowledge, and about the people who cannot stop caring.

## The Peasant Mirror

**Date:** 2021-05-22

**Title:** The Peasant Mirror

**Author:** Jeremias Gotthelf

**Themes:** Autobiography, Farmer, Vocation

## Synopsis: The Peasant Mirror — Jeremias Gotthelf

The Peasant Mirror presents itself as a life of Jeremias Gotthelf told by himself, a mirror held up to a peasant world and to the pastor-novelist who described it. Memory and example are the method.

The subtitle on some editions makes the self-portrait explicit. A reader gets the man and the parishes, not a disguised romance.

It is the right book if you want the author's own frame around the village novels.
