## Synopsis: How Joggeli seeks a wife

How Joggeli seeks a wife is a wooing story, Joggeli looking for a wife, in the Swiss village vein. The comedy of choosing a partner, with parents, farms, and pride in the room, is what the title promises.

No extraordinary crime is required. A marriage decision, for this kind of tale, is the adventure.

## Synopsis: Jacob's journeyman's wanderings through Switzerland

Jacob's journeyman's wanderings through Switzerland sends Jacob on a journeyman's wanderings through Switzerland, the old route by which a craftsman learned his trade away from home. The road is an education.

Landscapes and workshops matter. So does the temptation to become someone slipshod far from anyone who knows his name.

It is a travel book inside a moral tradition, concrete about work.

## Synopsis: Money and spirit — Jeremias Gotthelf

Money and spirit sets money and spirit against each other in a Gotthelf marriage story. A household can be ruined by meanness as surely as by extravagance, and he is interested in the piety that survives neither.

Reconciliation, where the subtitle names it, is not a mood. It is a change in how people hold property and each other.

The novel belongs to his long argument that a farm is a moral instrument.

**Author:** Jeremias Gotthelf

**Themes:** Teaching, Village, Vocation

## Synopsis: Sufferings and joys of a schoolmaster — Jeremias Gotthelf

Sufferings and joys of a schoolmaster follows a schoolmaster through the sufferings and joys named in the title. Gotthelf knew rural schools as places of low pay, high duty, and parents who have their own ideas.

The novel treats teaching as a calling that can be starved by the community it serves. There is affection for the work and no sentimentality about the conditions.

Part-editions divide the same life. The subject remains one man's classroom and the village around it.

**Title:** The cheese dairy in the Vehfreude: A story from Switzerland

**Author:** Jeremias Gotthelf

**Themes:** Farm, Cooperation, Pride

## Synopsis: The cheese dairy in the Vehfreude: A story from Switzerland — Jeremias Gotthelf

The cheese dairy in the Vehfreude: A story from Switzerland is Gotthelf's story of a cheese dairy in the Vehfreude, a cooperative undertaking that tests whether neighbors can share a craft without sharing one another's worst traits. Butter, account books, and pride are the plot.

He loves the technical life of a farm and distrusts any reform that ignores character. Both loves run here.

The book is local, exact, and surprisingly suspenseful about a dairy.

**Title:** Uli the tenant

**Author:** Jeremias Gotthelf

**Themes:** Farm, Responsibility, Marriage

## Synopsis: Uli the tenant — Jeremias Gotthelf

Uli the tenant is Gotthelf's later Uli book, when the servant has become a tenant and the harder test begins: land, debt, a household, and the character required to hold them. Getting the farm is not the same as deserving it.

Gotthelf remains practical and moral at once. A man's treatment of work, wife, and money is the plot.

It continues the story of a rise in the world, and it is less interested in the rise than in whether the man stays sound.

**Title:** Zeitgeist and Bernese Spirit

**Author:** Jeremias Gotthelf

**Themes:** Tradition, Faith, Change

## Synopsis: Zeitgeist and Bernese Spirit — Jeremias Gotthelf

Zeitgeist and Bernese Spirit sets the fashionable spirit of the age against a Bernese spirit Gotthelf thought more solid. He was a pastor who distrusted easy progress and wrote novels to argue.

The conflict is cultural: new ideas arriving in a countryside with its own hard-won habits. He is not neutral.

Reading him here means reading a conservative imagination at full strength, specific and alive.
