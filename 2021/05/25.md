# 2021-05-25 — Sufferings and joys of a schoolmaster: First part and Sufferings and joys of a schoolmaster: Part Two

## Sufferings and joys of a schoolmaster: First part

**Date:** 2021-05-25

**Title:** Sufferings and joys of a schoolmaster: First part

**Author:** Jeremias Gotthelf

**Themes:** Teaching, Village, Vocation

## Synopsis: Sufferings and joys of a schoolmaster: First part — Jeremias Gotthelf

Sufferings and joys of a schoolmaster: First part follows a schoolmaster through the sufferings and joys named in the title. Gotthelf knew rural schools as places of low pay, high duty, and parents who have their own ideas.

The novel treats teaching as a calling that can be starved by the community it serves. There is affection for the work and no sentimentality about the conditions.

Part-editions divide the same life. The subject remains one man's classroom and the village around it.
