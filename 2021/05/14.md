# 2021-05-14 — De Profundis, Salome, The Canterville Ghost, The Happy Prince, The selfish giant, and Three Essays

**Date:** 2021-05-14

**Title:** De Profundis

**Author:** Oscar Wilde

**Themes:** Prison, Art, Suffering

## Synopsis: De Profundis — Oscar Wilde

De Profundis is Oscar Wilde's long letter from prison, written after the collapse of his trial and his life in London. He examines love, vanity, Christ, and the art he thinks suffering might still make possible.

The addressee is the man whose relationship led to the downfall. Anger and tenderness share the pages.

It is a work of self-reckoning, ornate even in disgrace, and it does not pretend the disgrace was a misunderstanding.

**Date:** 2021-05-14

**Title:** Salome

**Author:** Oscar Wilde

**Themes:** Desire, Death, Performance

## Synopsis: Salome — Oscar Wilde

Salome is Oscar Wilde's play of Salome, Jokanaan, and a dance demanded as the price of a prophet's head. Moonlight, refusal, and a gaze that will not be denied drive a very short action.

The language is ritualized, almost a chant. Desire is staged as something that would rather destroy its object than lose it.

It is a theater piece of obsession, not a character study in the domestic sense.

**Date:** 2021-05-14

**Title:** The Canterville Ghost

**Themes:** Haunting, Humor, America

## Synopsis: The Canterville Ghost

The Canterville Ghost is Wilde's comedy of an old English ghost and the practical American family that buys his haunted house. Stains are cleaned with modern products. The ghost's professional pride suffers.

Under the jokes there is a lonely spirit and a girl willing to take him seriously. The satire aims at both Yankee efficiency and aristocratic gloom.

It is Wilde in a light mood, with a soft ending.

**Date:** 2021-05-14

**Title:** The Happy Prince

**Author:** Oscar Wilde

**Themes:** Generosity, City, Sacrifice

## Synopsis: The Happy Prince — Oscar Wilde

The Happy Prince is Wilde's fairy tale of a gilded statue who gives away his jewels, with the help of a swallow, to the poor of the city he can finally see. Beauty here is a fortune to be spent.

The officials who come afterward admire the wrong thing. The story's tenderness is blunt about who is valued.

It is a short tale that children follow for the bird and adults reread for the indictment.

**Date:** 2021-05-14

**Title:** The selfish giant

**Author:** Oscar Wilde

**Themes:** Selfishness, Garden, Child

## Synopsis: The selfish giant — Oscar Wilde

The selfish giant is Wilde's tale of a giant who walls his garden against children and finds that spring refuses to enter. One small boy changes the season and, later, the giant's idea of what the garden is for.

The religious close is open, not hidden. The story still works as a fable about possession.

It is among the briefest of his fairy tales, and one of the most remembered.

**Date:** 2021-05-14

**Title:** Three Essays

**Themes:** Essay, Ideas, Trio

## Synopsis: Three Essays

Three Essays is three essays, a small book of argument or reflection, and it names no author and no subjects. Three sustained tries at an idea are what the number promises.

Guessing the topics would replace this book with another. The form is the reliable fact.

It is a short book of thinking, proportioned to be finished.
