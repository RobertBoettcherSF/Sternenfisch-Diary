## Brasil

**Date:** 2021-05-30

**Title:** Brasil

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Exile, Future, Country

## Synopsis: Brasil — Stefan Zweig

Brasil is Stefan Zweig's essay on Brazil, written after he had left Europe. He presents the country as a possible future, a place not yet organized around the hatreds he was fleeing.

The portrait is hopeful and incomplete, the view of a newcomer who wants to believe in what he has found.

It belongs with the last part of his life, beside the memoir of the world he had lost.

**Themes:** Brazil, Hope, Exile

## Synopsis: Brasil: Uma terra do futuro — Stefan Zweig

## El mundo de ayer: Memorias de un europeo

**Date:** 2021-05-30

**Title:** El mundo de ayer: Memorias de un europeo

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Memory, Europe, Exile

## Synopsis: El mundo de ayer: Memorias de un europeo — Stefan Zweig

El mundo de ayer: Memorias de un europeo is Stefan Zweig's memoir of the Europe he lost, under this edition's title. The early pages are a grateful inventory of a cultured life. The later pages are the inventory's destruction.

War, dictatorship, and statelessness arrive as personal facts, not only as headlines. He writes from after the end.

The book is a farewell to a way of being a European, offered by someone who could not keep it.

## On War

**Date:** 2021-05-30

**Title:** On War

**Author:** Carl von Clausewitz

**Themes:** War, Strategy, Politics

## Synopsis: On War — Carl von Clausewitz

On War is Carl von Clausewitz's treatise on war as an instrument of policy, not as a separate sport of generals. Friction, uncertainty, and the famous formula that war is a continuation of politics are the ideas readers still come for.

The book is unfinished and uneven, a soldier thinking on the page. It rewards the chapters on courage, genius, and the defense as much as the quotable lines.

It is theory written by someone who had watched plans fail in the mud.

## Sobre la guerra

**Date:** 2021-05-30

**Title:** Sobre la guerra

**Author:** Carl von Clausewitz

**Themes:** War, Policy, Friction

## Synopsis: Sobre la guerra — Carl von Clausewitz

Sobre la guerra is Clausewitz's On War in the language of this edition. He treats war as political activity continued by other means, and he is relentless about friction, the small real things that make plans lie.

The book is a classic because later soldiers could not get out from under its questions. It is unfinished and still more coherent than most finished theories.

Read it for the thinking about uncertainty, not for a story of one battle.

## The Broom Maker of Rychiswyl

**Date:** 2021-05-30

**Title:** The Broom Maker of Rychiswyl

**Author:** Jeremias Gotthelf

**Themes:** Craft, Integrity, Village

## Synopsis: The Broom Maker of Rychiswyl — Jeremias Gotthelf

The Broom Maker of Rychiswyl names the broom-maker of Rychiswyl, a craftsman known by a humble trade and a place. Stories under this title turn on whether a small, honest skill can hold its ground among people with more status.

No author is listed. The moral shape is still visible in the trade: brooms, daily work, a man defined by usefulness.

It is a village portrait with a working pair of hands at the center.
