**Author:** ステファン・ツヴァイク

**Themes:** Memory, Europe, Exile

## Synopsis: 昨日の世界 — ステファン・ツヴァイク

昨日の世界 is Stefan Zweig's memoir of the Europe he lost, under this edition's title. The early pages are a grateful inventory of a cultured life. The later pages are the inventory's destruction.

War, dictatorship, and statelessness arrive as personal facts, not only as headlines. He writes from after the end.

The book is a farewell to a way of being a European, offered by someone who could not keep it.
