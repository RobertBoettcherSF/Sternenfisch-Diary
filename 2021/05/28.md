## De wereld van gisteren: Memoires van een Europeaan (The World of Yesterday: Memoirs of a European)

**Date:** 2021-05-28

**Title:** De wereld van gisteren: Memoires van een Europeaan (The World of Yesterday: Memoirs of a European)

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Language:** Dutch

**Themes:** Memory, Europe, Exile

## Synopsis: De wereld van gisteren: Memoires van een Europeaan (The World of Yesterday: Memoirs of a European) — Stefan Zweig

De wereld van gisteren: Memoires van een Europeaan is Stefan Zweig's memoir of the Europe he lost, under this edition's title. The early pages are a grateful inventory of a cultured life. The later pages are the inventory's destruction.

War, dictatorship, and statelessness arrive as personal facts, not only as headlines. He writes from after the end.

The book is a farewell to a way of being a European, offered by someone who could not keep it.
