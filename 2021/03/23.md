**Themes:** House, Perception, Uncanny

## Synopsis: The enchanted house — Robert Musil

The enchanted house is Robert Musil's 'enchanted house,' a prose work in which a dwelling stops behaving like neutral scenery. Musil's habit is analysis: feelings taken apart until they look stranger, not a ghost story with a explained chain.

A reliable scene-by-scene plot is not something to fake from the title. The promise is a house and a mind observing it too exactly.

Readers of The Man Without Qualities will recognize the temperature, on a much smaller scale.
