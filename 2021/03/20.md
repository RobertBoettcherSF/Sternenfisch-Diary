**Author:** Étienne de La Boëtie

**Themes:** Tyranny, Consent, Freedom

## Synopsis: Of the voluntary servitude of man — Étienne de La Boëtie

Of the voluntary servitude of man is Étienne de La Boétie's discourse on why people obey a tyrant they could refuse. His unsettling claim is that servitude is largely voluntary, held up by custom, favor, and the chain of those who profit just below the top.

The remedy he imagines is not a battle so much as a withdrawal of support. He writes as a young humanist, sharp and astonished.

The essay is short and has had a long life among readers thinking about power that does not need to persuade.
