## Maria Stuart (Mary Stuart)

**Date:** 2021-03-30

**Title:** Maria Stuart (Mary Stuart)

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Language:** German

**Themes:** Queens, Rivalry, History

## Synopsis: Maria Stuart (Mary Stuart) — Stefan Zweig

Maria Stuart is Stefan Zweig's life of Mary, Queen of Scots, written against the long shadow of Elizabeth. The book follows a queen whose marriages, religion, and claim to a neighboring throne made her both romantic and dangerous.

Captivity in England occupies as much of the story as the Scottish reign. Zweig watches a political prisoner become a symbol, and a symbol become a sentence.

The subject is royal charisma under guard, and the use two kingdoms made of one woman's name.

## Trip to Russia

**Date:** 2021-03-30

**Title:** Trip to Russia

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Russia, Travel, Writers

## Synopsis: Trip to Russia — Stefan Zweig

Trip to Russia is Zweig's account of a trip to Russia, curious, literary, and personal. He goes as a European man of letters meeting a world he has mostly known through its novels.

The value is the traveler's eye, not a political system explained. Encounters and atmospheres do the work.

It belongs with his other travel pages, before exile made travel a different kind of fact.
