**Date:** 2021-03-24

**Title:** Five little prose pieces

**Author:** Kurt Tucholsky

**Themes:** Prose, Short, Pieces

## Synopsis: Five little prose pieces — Kurt Tucholsky

Five little prose pieces promises five little prose pieces, a small book of short forms. No author, no subjects, and therefore no invented topics.

The appeal is scale. Five tries, brief, meant to be read as pieces rather than as chapters of one emergency.

It suits a reader who likes to finish something before the tea cools.

**Title:** Forgotten dreams

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Dream, Memory, Youth

## Synopsis: Forgotten dreams — Stefan Zweig

Forgotten dreams belongs with Zweig's early tales of dreams and first feelings, the sort of story in which a forgotten dream returns with the force of a fact. The scale is small and psychological.

He was already good at the confession that starts too late. That structure matters more than a list of scenes.

Readers of his mature novellas will see the seed.

**Title:** Jonathan's Dictionary

**Author:** Kurt Tucholsky

**Themes:** Words, Dictionary, Character

## Synopsis: Jonathan's Dictionary — Kurt Tucholsky

Jonathan's Dictionary is Jonathan's dictionary, a book of words owned by a Jonathan, or a portrait of a man made out of his definitions. No author is given, so the dictionary will not be filled here with sample entries.

The charm of such a title is lexicography as character. Someone believes the world can be sorted into meanings.

It may be playful and it may be earnest. The title allows both, and the pages decide.

**Title:** Open letter to Maximilian Harden

**Author:** Rainer Maria Rilke

**Themes:** Letter, Public, Poetry

## Synopsis: Open letter to Maximilian Harden — Rainer Maria Rilke

Open letter to Maximilian Harden is Rilke's open letter to Maximilian Harden, a public answer rather than a private note. Harden was a formidable journalist. A letter addressed to him is meant to be overheard.

The text is occasional. Its value is the stance of a poet speaking in a controversy, not a plot.

It belongs with Rilke's public prose, beside the essays on art and on Rodin.

**Title:** The crypt guardian

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Tomb, Watch, Fragment

## Synopsis: The crypt guardian — Franz Kafka

The crypt guardian is Kafka's short dramatic fragment about a watch kept at a tomb. The situation is stark: a guard, a grave, and a duty that may matter more to the guard than to anyone he is protecting.

Little of a full plot survives. What the pages offer is the pressure of a post that cannot be left.

It reads as a sketch of vigilance, not as a finished play.

**Date:** 2021-03-24

**Title:** The star over the forest

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Love, Forest, Novella

## Synopsis: The star over the forest — Stefan Zweig

The star over the forest is an early Zweig novella named for a star over a forest, a tale of youthful love set apart from the city. The mood is lyrical and already fated.

Two people, a landscape, and a feeling too large for their circumstances are the elements. A detailed intrigue is unnecessary to the spell.

It shows the romantic register he later tightened but did not entirely leave.
