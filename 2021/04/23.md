## An Old heart goes on a journey: Novel

**Date:** 2021-04-23

**Title:** An Old heart goes on a journey: Novel

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Age, Journey, Kindness

## Synopsis: An Old heart goes on a journey: Novel — Hans Fallada

An Old heart goes on a journey: Novel is Fallada's late story of an old heart that goes traveling. An elderly person sets out, and the journey is a moral experiment in what age still owes the young and itself.

It is warmer than the inflation novels, without becoming soft about need. Fallada still counts the coins.

The appeal is a single, late act of decency given room to matter.

## Anderswo: Little stories (Elsewhere: Little Stories)

**Date:** 2021-04-23

**Title:** Anderswo: Little stories (Elsewhere: Little Stories)

**Author:** Kurt Tucholsky

**Language:** German

**Themes:** Sketch, Place, Wit

## Synopsis: Anderswo: Little stories (Elsewhere: Little Stories) — Kurt Tucholsky

Anderswo: Little stories takes the German word for elsewhere as its title. Tucholsky's short prose often works that way: a scene, a type, a small injustice, caught from a seat that is not quite at home.

Some editions add that these are little stories. They are glances, not epics.

The pleasure is the voice, quick and personally responsible for its jokes.

## Reviews 1902 - 1939

**Date:** 2021-04-23

**Title:** Reviews 1902 - 1939

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Books, Criticism, Reading

## Synopsis: Reviews 1902 - 1939 — Stefan Zweig

Reviews 1902 - 1939 collects Stefan Zweig's reviews and literary encounters across the years named in the title. The pieces are responses to books and to the writers who made them, written as a working reader rather than as a system.

Taken together they sketch a taste: European, biographical, impatient with dullness, loyal to intensity.

It is a book for someone who likes criticism that still sounds like a person talking.

## Romain Rolland

**Date:** 2021-04-23

**Title:** Romain Rolland

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Biography, Pacifism, Music

## Synopsis: Romain Rolland — Stefan Zweig

Romain Rolland is Stefan Zweig's portrait of Romain Rolland, the French writer and music scholar he admired as a European conscience. The life and the stance against nationalist hatred are the subject.

Some editions add that this is the man and the work. That double attention is Zweig's usual method: the books inside the biography.

It is an essay of friendship as much as of criticism.

## Sparrow Farm: Tale of the town clerk who flew to the countryside

**Date:** 2021-04-23

**Title:** Sparrow Farm: Tale of the town clerk who flew to the countryside

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Country, Escape, Clerk

## Synopsis: Sparrow Farm: Tale of the town clerk who flew to the countryside — Hans Fallada

Sparrow Farm: Tale of the town clerk who flew to the countryside announces itself in the subtitle as the tale of a town clerk who fled to the countryside. Fallada's interest in that flight is practical: what a small holding demands, and what a city person imagines it will cure.

The note will not add scenes beyond that framing. A clerk, a rural experiment, and the hope of a different life are the story the title offers.

It sits with his other books about ordinary people trying to change the terms of their work.

## The drunkard: Novel

**Date:** 2021-04-23

**Title:** The drunkard: Novel

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Alcohol, Decline, Confession

## Synopsis: The drunkard: Novel — Hans Fallada

The drunkard: Novel is Fallada's novel of a businessman who slides into drink and watches his competence, marriage, and freedom go in that order. The narration has the clarity of someone describing a trap from inside it.

Institutions, a clinic, a spell of punishment, do not supply a moral miracle. The interest is the mechanism of a decline.

Fallada writes it without romantic blur. That dryness is the book's mercy and its harshness.

## The great moments of humanity: Twelve historical miniatures

**Date:** 2021-04-23

**Title:** The great moments of humanity: Twelve historical miniatures

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** History, Decision, Destiny

## Synopsis: The great moments of humanity: Twelve historical miniatures — Stefan Zweig

The great moments of humanity: Twelve historical miniatures collects Stefan Zweig's miniature histories, each built around a moment he thought turned a larger fate. A discovery, a defeat, a decision made in an hour: the form is the short narrative essay.

The pleasure is compression. A battle or a voyage is reduced to the instant where it could still have gone otherwise.

It is history written for readers who want the hinge, not the whole campaign.

## The nightmare: Novel

**Date:** 2021-04-23

**Title:** The nightmare: Novel

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Fear, Novel, Pressure

## Synopsis: The nightmare: Novel — Hans Fallada

The nightmare: Novel is a Hans Fallada novel whose title announces a nightmare, a mind or a household under a fear that will not stay in sleep. Inventing the apparatus of that fear would be a disservice.

His novels typically put a private dread next to rent, work, and neighbors. A reader can expect that double pressure here.

The book asks to be met on the page, where the nightmare is specified.

## Three masters: Balzac - Dickens - Dostoevsky

**Date:** 2021-04-23

**Title:** Three masters: Balzac - Dickens - Dostoevsky

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Writers, Biography, Craft

## Synopsis: Three masters: Balzac - Dickens - Dostoevsky — Stefan Zweig

Three masters: Balzac - Dickens - Dostoevsky is Stefan Zweig's set of portraits of Balzac, Dickens, and Dostoevsky. He treats each novelist as a kind of excess: the builder of a world, the celebrant of ordinary hearts, the examiner of guilt.

The essays are not plot summaries. They look for the pressure that made the work unavoidable.

Read together, they are a small map of the nineteenth-century novel as three temperaments.
