**Title:** Chess novella

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Chess, Captivity, Obsession

## Synopsis: Chess novella — Stefan Zweig

Chess novella is Stefan Zweig's novella about a game that becomes a prison. On an ocean liner, a crude world champion meets a stranger whose only training was a chess book memorized in solitary confinement.

The match is short. The real story is how a mind survives when the only opponent left is itself, and what happens when that practice is dragged back into daylight.

Zweig wrote it late, and the cruelty of confinement is the point, not the opening theory.

## Collected Stories

**Date:** 2021-04-01

**Title:** Collected Stories

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Stories, Novella, Character

## Synopsis: Collected Stories — Stefan Zweig

Collected Stories gathers Stefan Zweig's stories, the form in which he did much of his best work. A life is often caught at the moment it loses proportion: a passion, a disgrace, a confession on a journey.

The moonlight-alley subtitle on one edition points to that nocturnal, urban mood. Other pieces range wider, but the unit is the intense short tale.

It is a reading book, one story at a sitting, rather than a single arc.
