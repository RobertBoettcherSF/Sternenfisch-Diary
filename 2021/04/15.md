## Gripsholm castle: A summer story

**Date:** 2021-04-15

**Title:** Gripsholm castle: A summer story

**Author:** Kurt Tucholsky

**Themes:** Summer, Holiday, Love

## Synopsis: Gripsholm castle: A summer story — Kurt Tucholsky

Gripsholm castle: A summer story is Tucholsky's summer story of a holiday at Gripsholm, a playful escape shared by lovers and a friend. The castle is a toy setting for a few weeks of chosen leisure.

The book knows that a holiday is a fiction people agree to inhabit. It enjoys the fiction and notices the edges.

Reading it is like being allowed into a private joke that remains kind.

## Letters to Ottla and the Family: Excerpts

**Date:** 2021-04-15

**Title:** Letters to Ottla and the Family: Excerpts

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Family, Letters, Sister

## Synopsis: Letters to Ottla and the Family: Excerpts — Franz Kafka

Letters to Ottla and the Family: Excerpts collects Kafka's letters to his sister Ottla and to the family around her. They are more intimate and more practical than the fiction: health, apartments, the war, and the relief of writing to someone who took his side.

Ottla is the relative with whom he could be least defended. The letters show affection, advice, and the ordinary logistics of staying close.

Read beside the letter to his father, they are the other temperature of the same household.

## Letters to writers

**Date:** 2021-04-15

**Title:** Letters to writers

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Letters, Literature, Friendship

## Synopsis: Letters to writers — Stefan Zweig

Letters to writers gathers Stefan Zweig's correspondence with other writers. The interest is the working life of letters: encouragement, disagreement, exile, and the practical business of books.

These are not a single narrative. They show a man who lived through other people's manuscripts as well as his own.

What a browser finds is the tone of a literary friendship, attentive and often urgent.
