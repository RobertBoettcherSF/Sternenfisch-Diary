## Amerigo

**Date:** 2021-04-14

**Title:** Amerigo

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Exploration, Name, America

## Synopsis: Amerigo — Stefan Zweig

Amerigo is Stefan Zweig's portrait of Amerigo Vespucci, the man whose first name was given to two continents. Zweig is interested in how a navigator's letters became a baptism for a world.

The drama is reputation as much as seamanship. A name sticks, and the sticking is a historical event of its own.

It is a short biographical essay in Zweig's swift manner, about fame and the map.

## Gripsholm castle

**Date:** 2021-04-14

**Title:** Gripsholm castle

**Author:** Kurt Tucholsky

**Themes:** Summer, Holiday, Love

## Synopsis: Gripsholm castle — Kurt Tucholsky

Gripsholm castle is Tucholsky's summer story of a holiday at Gripsholm, a playful escape shared by lovers and a friend. The castle is a toy setting for a few weeks of chosen leisure.

The book knows that a holiday is a fiction people agree to inhabit. It enjoys the fiction and notices the edges.

Reading it is like being allowed into a private joke that remains kind.

## Letters to Ottla and the Family

**Date:** 2021-04-14

**Title:** Letters to Ottla and the Family

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Family, Letters, Sister

## Synopsis: Letters to Ottla and the Family — Franz Kafka

Letters to Ottla and the Family collects Kafka's letters to his sister Ottla and to the family around her. They are more intimate and more practical than the fiction: health, apartments, the war, and the relief of writing to someone who took his side.

Ottla is the relative with whom he could be least defended. The letters show affection, advice, and the ordinary logistics of staying close.

Read beside the letter to his father, they are the other temperature of the same household.

## Short texts about musicians and visual artists

**Date:** 2021-04-14

**Title:** Short texts about musicians and visual artists

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Music, Art, Essay

## Synopsis: Short texts about musicians and visual artists — Stefan Zweig

Short texts about musicians and visual artists collects Zweig's short texts on musicians and visual artists. He writes as a passionate listener and looker, tying the work to the life without pretending to be a cataloger.

The essays are invitations. A composer or a painter is approached through the pressure that made the art.

They sit beside his literary portraits as part of one lifelong attention to makers.

## Stories from the estate

**Date:** 2021-04-14

**Title:** Stories from the estate

**Author:** Rainer Maria Rilke

**Themes:** Stories, Estate, Prose

## Synopsis: Stories from the estate — Rainer Maria Rilke

Stories from the estate draws on stories from Rilke's estate, prose he did not necessarily finish for a bookstall. The pieces have his usual pressure: a childhood, a city, a figure seen too intensely.

Because they are estate papers, a reader should expect fragments beside finished tales. That unfinishedness is honest to the source.

They add rooms to the work known from Malte and the New Poems, not a second life.

## Stories of the dear God

**Date:** 2021-04-14

**Title:** Stories of the dear God

**Author:** Rainer Maria Rilke

**Themes:** Parable, Faith, Story

## Synopsis: Stories of the dear God — Rainer Maria Rilke

Stories of the dear God collects Rilke's Stories of the Dear God, tales told as if to explain God to a listener who might be a child or a skeptic. Neighbors, artists, and small events carry the theology.

The manner is gentle and odd. A story may end by shifting who has been speaking.

They are among his most approachable pages, and they are not simple.

## The invisible collection

**Date:** 2021-04-14

**Title:** The invisible collection

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Art, Collecting, Loss

## Synopsis: The invisible collection — Stefan Zweig

The invisible collection is Zweig's story of a collector who will not admit that his treasures are gone. During the inflation, a visitor is shown a room of master drawings that are no longer on the wall.

Pride, ruin, and the kindness of people who agree to see what is not there make the tale. It is short and sharp.

The subject is how a person protects a life by refusing the evidence.

## The Stories

**Date:** 2021-04-14

**Title:** The Stories

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Stories, Fiction, Parable

## Synopsis: The Stories — Franz Kafka

The Stories is a gathering of Franz Kafka's short fiction rather than a single plot. The pieces range from parables of a few lines to stories in which an ordinary errand opens onto a procedure with no exit.

Animals, officials, sons, and messengers recur. The tone can be comic for a page and then refuse to release the joke.

A reader new to Kafka can start here and meet the method without committing to one of the unfinished novels.

## Travels in Europe

**Date:** 2021-04-14

**Title:** Travels in Europe

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Travel, Europe, Observation

## Synopsis: Travels in Europe — Stefan Zweig

Travels in Europe follows Stefan Zweig's travels in Europe, the continent he treated as a single cultural home. Cities, meetings, and the pleasure of crossing borders without drama fill the pages.

The later knowledge that those borders would harden gives the journeys a shadow they did not all have at the time. The book can be read for the delight alone.

It is prose of movement, by a writer who thought civilization was a way of visiting one another.

## Twelve Tales

**Date:** 2021-04-14

**Title:** Twelve Tales

**Author:** Rainer Maria Rilke

**Themes:** Tales, Youth, Prose

## Synopsis: Twelve Tales — Rainer Maria Rilke

Twelve Tales is a set of twelve tales associated with the early Rilke, short prose with a taste for legend, childhood, and the uncanny edge of a real street. Each can stand alone.

The manner is more narrative than the later poems, and already unwilling to be merely pretty.

They are a good way to meet his prose without starting at Malte's density.

## The Stories: Part II

**Date:** 2021-04-14

**Title:** The Stories: Part II

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Stories, Selection, Fiction

## Synopsis: The Stories: Part II — Franz Kafka

The Stories: Part II is a second gathering of Franz Kafka's stories, a sequel selection rather than one continuous plot. Which tales are bound in depends on the editor, and that table of contents will not be guessed.

What does not change is the kind of piece: short fiction in which an errand, an animal, or a sentence of law opens onto something that will not resolve politely.

It is a reading volume. Each story ends, and the unease does not always agree that it has ended.
