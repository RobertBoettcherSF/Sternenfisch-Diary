## About writers

**Date:** 2021-04-06

**Title:** About writers

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Writers, Essay, Reading

## Synopsis: About writers — Stefan Zweig

About writers is Stefan Zweig on writers, critical and biographical prose rather than his letters to them. He approaches an author as a fate that found a style.

The pieces are encounters, not a system of literary theory. Warmth and drama lead.

Readers who like his portraits of Balzac or Dickens will recognize the method, aimed here at a shelf of other lives.

## America: Novel

**Date:** 2021-04-06

**Title:** America: Novel

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** America, Youth, Exile

## Synopsis: America: Novel — Franz Kafka

America: Novel is Franz Kafka's unfinished novel of a European boy pushed out to America. Karl Rossmann arrives already in disgrace and moves through a country of hotels, elevators, and employers who rename him.

The book breaks off before a settled ending. What exists is a comic and anxious tour of work, patronage, and the feeling of being permanently new.

It is the lightest of Kafka's novels in scenery and still a story about not being let in.

## Collected Stories: The moonlight alley

**Date:** 2021-04-06

**Title:** Collected Stories: The moonlight alley

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Stories, Novella, Character

## Synopsis: Collected Stories: The moonlight alley — Stefan Zweig

Collected Stories: The moonlight alley gathers Stefan Zweig's stories, the form in which he did much of his best work. A life is often caught at the moment it loses proportion: a passion, a disgrace, a confession on a journey.

The moonlight-alley subtitle on one edition points to that nocturnal, urban mood. Other pieces range wider, but the unit is the intense short tale.

It is a reading book, one story at a sitting, rather than a single arc.

## Impatience of the heart

**Date:** 2021-04-06

**Title:** Impatience of the heart

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Pity, Duty, Shame

## Synopsis: Impatience of the heart — Stefan Zweig

Impatience of the heart is the novel Zweig also called a warning about pity. A young Austrian officer shows kindness to a disabled girl and then cannot tell help from love, or escape the role her family builds around him.

Good intentions tighten. The book is severe about sentiment that enjoys its own virtue and will not pay the ordinary price of honesty.

It ends as a tragedy of someone who wanted to be gentle and became cruel by refusing to stop.

## Intoxication of transformation

**Date:** 2021-04-06

**Title:** Intoxication of transformation

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Love, Poverty, Transformation

## Synopsis: Intoxication of transformation — Stefan Zweig

Intoxication of transformation is the novel published in English as The Post-Office Girl and in German as the intoxication of transformation. A young woman is briefly lifted into a life of hotels and ease, then sent back, and the return poisons what she can bear.

Zweig studies resentment as a chemical result of a glimpse. Love and crime become imaginable in the same lowered room.

The book is harsher than his nostalgic essays. It knows what a short holiday of wealth can do.

## Maria Stuart

**Date:** 2021-04-06

**Title:** Maria Stuart

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Queens, Rivalry, History

## Synopsis: Maria Stuart — Stefan Zweig

Maria Stuart is Stefan Zweig's life of Mary, Queen of Scots, written against the long shadow of Elizabeth. The book follows a queen whose marriages, religion, and claim to a neighboring throne made her both romantic and dangerous.

Captivity in England occupies as much of the story as the Scottish reign. Zweig watches a political prisoner become a symbol, and a symbol become a sentence.

The subject is royal charisma under guard, and the use two kingdoms made of one woman's name.

## Marie Antoinette: Portrait of a middle character

**Date:** 2021-04-06

**Title:** Marie Antoinette: Portrait of a middle character

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Court, Revolution, Portrait

## Synopsis: Marie Antoinette: Portrait of a middle character — Stefan Zweig

Marie Antoinette: Portrait of a middle character is Stefan Zweig's life of Marie Antoinette, from the Austrian child sent to Versailles to the woman tried by the Revolution. He is interested in the person inside the legend of luxury and indifference.

The court, the pamphlets, the flight to Varennes, and the prison are stages in a character he watches harden and clarify. Sympathy does not cancel the blindness of the old court.

It is a portrait of a queen formed by ceremony and then stripped of it.

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Novella, Passion, Fate

## Synopsis: Novellas and stories — Stefan Zweig

Novellas and stories is a collection of Zweig's novellas and stories, the genre he used for lives that tip. The pieces are independent. What they share is a crisis told at close range.

Travel, music, shame, and sudden confession recur. He likes a frame: someone telling the tale after the damage.

A new reader can enter anywhere. Each story is built to be finished in an evening.

## The Castle: Novel

**Date:** 2021-04-06

**Title:** The Castle: Novel

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Authority, Village, Access

## Synopsis: The Castle: Novel — Franz Kafka

The Castle: Novel follows K., a land surveyor who may or may not have been summoned, as he tries to reach the castle that governs a village. Officials sleep, messengers lose letters, and the village treats him as a problem to be housed.

Kafka leaves the book unfinished. The movement is not toward a solution but toward a closer and more humiliating intimacy with an authority that will not appear.

It is a novel about access: who is allowed to count as employed, known, and real.

## The healing through the spirit

**Date:** 2021-04-06

**Title:** The healing through the spirit

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Healing, Faith, Biography

## Synopsis: The healing through the spirit — Stefan Zweig

The healing through the spirit looks at three figures who claimed a power to heal through spirit rather than through ordinary medicine: Mesmer, Mary Baker Eddy, and Freud. Zweig sets magnetism, Christian Science, and psychoanalysis side by side.

He is interested in the authority a suffering person grants to a healer, and in the theories built to explain that authority.

The essays are skeptical and absorbed at once. They ask what cure means when the instrument is belief.

## The Trial: Novel

**Date:** 2021-04-06

**Title:** The Trial: Novel

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Law, Guilt, Bureaucracy

## Synopsis: The Trial: Novel — Franz Kafka

The Trial: Novel opens with an arrest that names no charge. Josef K. spends the rest of the book trying to find the court that has claimed him, in attics, stairwells, and conversations that explain nothing usable.

Every attempt to be reasonable feeds the procedure. Helpers, women, a painter, and a priest offer interpretations that do not free him.

The novel is about a guilt that arrives before the reason, and about offices that exist to prolong the case.

## The world of yesterday: Memories of a European

**Date:** 2021-04-06

**Title:** The world of yesterday: Memories of a European

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Memory, Europe, Exile

## Synopsis: The world of yesterday: Memories of a European — Stefan Zweig

The world of yesterday: Memories of a European is Stefan Zweig's memoir of the European culture he grew up in and then watched come apart. Vienna before 1914, the habits of reading and travel, and the friendships of a literary life fill the early pages.

The later chapters follow war, inflation, the rise of dictatorship, and the loss of a passport that once opened borders. Zweig writes as a man already outside the world he is describing.

It is a book about a civilization remembered from exile, not a chronicle of battles. The tone is grateful and bereaved at once.

## Wolf among wolves

**Date:** 2021-04-06

**Title:** Wolf among wolves

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Inflation, Survival, Berlin

## Synopsis: Wolf among wolves — Hans Fallada

Wolf among wolves is Fallada's long novel of the German inflation, when money died by the hour and character was priced accordingly. Farmers, officers, addicts, and schemers cross in a plot of survival and revenge.

The city and the country are both unsafe. Love and loyalty are tested against the simple need to eat next week.

The two parts, city and countryside, are one argument about a society that has lost its measure.
