## About writers

**Date:** 2021-04-07

**Title:** About writers

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Writers, Essay, Reading

## Synopsis: About writers — Stefan Zweig

About writers is Stefan Zweig on writers, critical and biographical prose rather than his letters to them. He approaches an author as a fate that found a style.

The pieces are encounters, not a system of literary theory. Warmth and drama lead.

Readers who like his portraits of Balzac or Dickens will recognize the method, aimed here at a shelf of other lives.

## Intoxication of transformation: Novel from the estate

**Date:** 2021-04-07

**Title:** Intoxication of transformation: Novel from the estate

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Love, Poverty, Transformation

## Synopsis: Intoxication of transformation: Novel from the estate — Stefan Zweig

Intoxication of transformation: Novel from the estate is the novel published in English as The Post-Office Girl and in German as the intoxication of transformation. A young woman is briefly lifted into a life of hotels and ease, then sent back, and the return poisons what she can bear.

Zweig studies resentment as a chemical result of a glimpse. Love and crime become imaginable in the same lowered room.

The book is harsher than his nostalgic essays. It knows what a short holiday of wealth can do.

## The world of yesterday

**Date:** 2021-04-07

**Title:** The world of yesterday

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Memory, Europe, Exile

## Synopsis: The world of yesterday — Stefan Zweig

The world of yesterday is Stefan Zweig's memoir of the European culture he grew up in and then watched come apart. Vienna before 1914, the habits of reading and travel, and the friendships of a literary life fill the early pages.

The later chapters follow war, inflation, the rise of dictatorship, and the loss of a passport that once opened borders. Zweig writes as a man already outside the world he is describing.

It is a book about a civilization remembered from exile, not a chronicle of battles. The tone is grateful and bereaved at once.

## Wolf among wolves: First part -The city and its restless

**Date:** 2021-04-07

**Title:** Wolf among wolves: First part -The city and its restless

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Inflation, Survival, Berlin

## Synopsis: Wolf among wolves: First part -The city and its restless — Hans Fallada

Wolf among wolves: First part -The city and its restless is Fallada's long novel of the German inflation, when money died by the hour and character was priced accordingly. Farmers, officers, addicts, and schemers cross in a plot of survival and revenge.

The city and the country are both unsafe. Love and loyalty are tested against the simple need to eat next week.

The two parts, city and countryside, are one argument about a society that has lost its measure.
