# 2021-04-09 — 16 satires, Aphorisms, In the penal colony, Rodin, and The burrow

**Date:** 2021-04-09

**Title:** 16 satires

**Author:** Kurt Tucholsky

**Themes:** Satire, Politics, Wit

## Synopsis: 16 satires — Kurt Tucholsky

16 satires collects satires by Kurt Tucholsky, who spent the Weimar years aiming jokes at militarism, stuffiness, and the respectable face of cruelty. The pieces are short and meant to sting in public.

He can be tender about ordinary people and savage about their leaders in the same afternoon. That split is the moral.

They are best read as interventions, each one dated by its target and still sharp about the type.

**Date:** 2021-04-09

**Title:** Aphorisms

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Aphorism, Faith, Paradox

## Synopsis: Aphorisms — Franz Kafka

Aphorisms collects Kafka's short reflective sentences, including the late aphorisms in which belief, the Law, and the self argue in a few lines. They are not explanations of the stories.

Each one is a small trap. A comforting reading usually fails by the last clause.

The book is for readers who want Kafka's thought without a plot to hide inside.

**Date:** 2021-04-09

**Title:** In the penal colony

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Punishment, Justice, Machine

## Synopsis: In the penal colony — Franz Kafka

In the penal colony is Kafka's story of a penal colony where the sentence is written into the body by a machine. An explorer is invited to watch an execution and to admire an apparatus its officer loves more than he loves the condemned man.

The old commander is dead, the machine is failing, and the officer cannot bear a world that has stopped believing in it.

The horror is procedural. Cruelty is presented as a beautiful tradition.

**Date:** 2021-04-09

**Title:** Rodin

**Author:** Rainer Maria Rilke

**Themes:** Sculpture, Body, Work

## Synopsis: Rodin — Rainer Maria Rilke

Rodin names Rodin. Without an author, the book may be life, criticism, or a portfolio in prose of the sculptor who made bodies look as if they were still deciding to move.

The subject is secure even when the genre is not. Bronze, clay, labor, and the modern figure are what the name puts on the table.

A reader comes for the artist. The pages say who is speaking about him.

**Date:** 2021-04-09

**Title:** The burrow

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Fear, Shelter, Obsession

## Synopsis: The burrow — Franz Kafka

The burrow is Kafka's late story of a creature that has built an elaborate burrow and cannot trust it. Every silence might be an intruder. Every improvement to the defenses creates a new weakness to listen for.

There is almost no outside event. The drama is attention itself, wearing a home down by guarding it.

It is a study of safety as a full-time fear.
