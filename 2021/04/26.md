## Anton and Gerda

**Date:** 2021-04-26

**Title:** Anton and Gerda

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Youth, Pair, Province

## Synopsis: Anton and Gerda — Hans Fallada

Anton and Gerda names Anton and Gerda, and it is one of Hans Fallada's early novels of a young pair in a provincial world. Public detail of the plot is thinner than for his famous later books, so this note will not invent episodes.

What can be said is the scale: two young people, a German town, and Fallada's habit of watching money and affection in the same scene.

Readers who know Little Man, What Now? will recognize the human size, in an earlier key.

## Fridolin the cheeky badger

**Date:** 2021-04-26

**Title:** Fridolin the cheeky badger

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Children, Animals, Mischief

## Synopsis: Fridolin the cheeky badger — Hans Fallada

Fridolin the cheeky badger is a children's story by Hans Fallada about a cheeky badger. The subtitle on some editions adds a two-legged figure as well as a four-legged one, which is enough to see the joke: animal manners and human ones sharing a tale.

It is not one of his grim novels of money. The promise is mischief, a wood, and a character who will not stay polite.

Adults who know only the Berlin books may be surprised by how light his hand can be.

## Legends

**Date:** 2021-04-26

**Title:** Legends

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Legend, Faith, Story

## Synopsis: Legends — Stefan Zweig

Legends collects Stefan Zweig's retellings of legendary material, stories that turn on mercy, recognition, and a moral test. He uses old plots to ask what a person owes a stranger.

The manner is clear and dramatic, closer to a novella than to a sermon.

They suit a reader who wants a short tale with a consequence.

## Magellan

**Date:** 2021-04-26

**Title:** Magellan

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Voyage, Obsession, Discovery

## Synopsis: Magellan — Stefan Zweig

Magellan follows Ferdinand Magellan's attempt to reach the Spice Islands by sailing west, and the voyage that became the first circumnavigation. Zweig treats the captain as a man consumed by a route other people called impossible.

Mutiny, hunger, a strait at the bottom of the map, and a death in the Philippines leave the ships to finish what their commander began. The achievement is told as endurance more than as triumph.

The book is about the cost of a fixed idea, and about the sailors who paid it.

## Pflege im 21ten Jahrhundert (Care in the 21st Century)

**Date:** 2021-04-26

**Title:** Pflege im 21ten Jahrhundert (Care in the 21st Century)

**Author:** Robert Boettcher

**Language:** German

**Themes:** Care, Work, Century

## Synopsis: Pflege im 21ten Jahrhundert (Care in the 21st Century) — Robert Boettcher

Pflege im 21ten Jahrhundert is Robert Boettcher's book on care in the twenty-first century. The subject is the work of looking after people, set in present conditions rather than in a past ideal of the sickroom.

Care, in a title like this, means a practice: attention, responsibility, and the institutions around them. The book treats that practice as contemporary work.

It is a practical book. Its recommendations are the ones on the page, written for readers who do the work or live beside it.

## Stories from the Murkelei

**Date:** 2021-04-26

**Title:** Stories from the Murkelei

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Childhood, Region, Tale

## Synopsis: Stories from the Murkelei — Hans Fallada

Stories from the Murkelei is Hans Fallada's stories from the Murkelei, a country of his own naming, closer to memory and fable than to the Berlin of his big novels. The pieces are lighter, local, and odd.

He still notices how people actually behave. The softness is not emptiness.

It is a book of tales from a made place, meant to be visited one story at a time.

## The fight with the demon: Hölderlin - Kleist - Nietzsche

**Date:** 2021-04-26

**Title:** The fight with the demon: Hölderlin - Kleist - Nietzsche

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Genius, Madness, Poetry

## Synopsis: The fight with the demon: Hölderlin - Kleist - Nietzsche — Stefan Zweig

The fight with the demon: Hölderlin - Kleist - Nietzsche gathers Zweig's essays on Hölderlin, Kleist, and Nietzsche, three writers he saw as driven past the point where a life can stay intact. The 'demon' of the title is the force that makes the work and breaks the person.

Each essay follows the same curve: early promise, isolation, and a mind that will not settle for a manageable career.

It is criticism written as drama, about the price of an uncompromising inner life.

## Today at home

**Date:** 2021-04-26

**Title:** Today at home

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Childhood, Memory, Home

## Synopsis: Today at home — Hans Fallada

Today at home belongs to Hans Fallada's books of home, sketches of the household he came from and the one he later made. Anecdote, not thesis, is the unit.

The charm is specific: rooms, relatives, the small operations of a German domestic life across troubled decades. He does not pretend coziness is the whole truth.

These pages are memoir in pieces, approachable, and best read as reminiscence rather than as a novel with a single crisis.

## Triumph and Tragedy of Erasmus of Rotterdam

**Date:** 2021-04-26

**Title:** Triumph and Tragedy of Erasmus of Rotterdam

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Conscience, Tolerance, Reform

## Synopsis: Triumph and Tragedy of Erasmus of Rotterdam — Stefan Zweig

Triumph and Tragedy of Erasmus of Rotterdam sets Erasmus of Rotterdam against the religious war that his own century chose. Zweig presents him as a scholar who wanted reform without the bonfire, and who lost the argument to louder men.

The contrast, in the longer telling, is with fanaticism that would rather break the church than share a sentence. Erasmus keeps writing, qualifying, and hoping that civility can hold.

It is a biography of a moderate in a time that punished moderation, and a defense of the right to hesitate.

## Young gentleman - very big

**Date:** 2021-04-26

**Title:** Young gentleman - very big

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Youth, Ambition, Size

## Synopsis: Young gentleman - very big — Hans Fallada

Young gentleman - very big uses a joking title, a young gentleman who is 'very big,' for a Hans Fallada story of youth and self-importance. The exact comic mechanism is the book's, not something to sketch from the adjective alone.

Expect a provincial or small-city scale and a hero whose estimate of himself is part of the problem. That is Fallada's home ground.

The title smiles. The prose, if it is like his other work, will not only smile.
