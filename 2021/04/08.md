# 2021-04-08 — Blumfeld and Last Letters

## Blumfeld

**Date:** 2021-04-08

**Title:** Blumfeld

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Bachelor, Routine, Absurd

## Synopsis: Blumfeld — Franz Kafka

Blumfeld is Kafka's fragment about a careful bachelor who comes home to find two small celluloid balls bouncing of their own accord. He tries to fold the nuisance into an orderly life.

The comedy is in the effort to remain a respectable tenant of his own rooms. The story breaks off before the disturbance is explained.

It suits readers who like Kafka at his most deadpan.

## Last Letters

**Date:** 2021-04-08

**Title:** Last Letters

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Letters, Farewell, Voice

## Synopsis: Last Letters — Franz Kafka

Last Letters is last letters, a bundle at the end of a correspondence or a life. No author tells us whose hand. Farewell, explanation, and the things people write when they think there may not be another sheet are the feelings the title carries.

A specific deathbed or a specific lover will not be supplied. The form is the letter, and the time is late.

Such books are intimate by genre. They assume a reader willing to overhear.
