## Among other things in the Pyrenees

**Date:** 2021-04-24

**Title:** Among other things in the Pyrenees

**Author:** Kurt Tucholsky

**Themes:** Travel, Exile, Observation

## Synopsis: Among other things in the Pyrenees — Kurt Tucholsky

Among other things in the Pyrenees is Tucholsky writing from among other places, the Pyrenees included, in the years when Germany was ceasing to be a home. Travel notes, memory, and political weather share the pages.

The manner is still conversational. The lightness carries more freight than in Rheinsberg.

It is a book of a writer watching Europe from a temporary address.

## Reviews 1902 - 1939: Encounters with books

**Date:** 2021-04-24

**Title:** Reviews 1902 - 1939: Encounters with books

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Books, Criticism, Reading

## Synopsis: Reviews 1902 - 1939: Encounters with books — Stefan Zweig

Reviews 1902 - 1939: Encounters with books collects Stefan Zweig's reviews and literary encounters across the years named in the title. The pieces are responses to books and to the writers who made them, written as a working reader rather than as a system.

Taken together they sketch a taste: European, biographical, impatient with dullness, loyal to intensity.

It is a book for someone who likes criticism that still sounds like a person talking.

## Romain Rolland: The man and the work

**Date:** 2021-04-24

**Title:** Romain Rolland: The man and the work

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Biography, Pacifism, Music

## Synopsis: Romain Rolland: The man and the work — Stefan Zweig

Romain Rolland: The man and the work is Stefan Zweig's portrait of Romain Rolland, the French writer and music scholar he admired as a European conscience. The life and the stance against nationalist hatred are the subject.

Some editions add that this is the man and the work. That double attention is Zweig's usual method: the books inside the biography.

It is an essay of friendship as much as of criticism.
