Sea voyage is a sea voyage, one of the oldest containers for a story. Departure, weather, a ship as a society, and the hope of a landfall are implied by the words alone.

No author, no particular ocean, no invented storm. The genre is the promise.

Readers who want distance and a deck underfoot will know at once whether to open it.
