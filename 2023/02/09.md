## How could the world end?

**Date:** 2023-02-09

**Title:** How could the world end?

**Author:** Unknown

**Themes:** Ending, Speculation, World

## Synopsis: How could the world end? — Unknown

How could the world end? asks how the world could end. The question is the book. Cosmic, religious, scientific, or satirical answers are all compatible with the words, and none will be picked without evidence.

No author is named. What a reader can expect is an attempt to face finality rather than a domestic plot that happens to use a grand phrase.

The title is a speculation. The pages are the form that speculation takes.

## The Dead City

**Date:** 2023-02-09

**Title:** The Dead City

**Author:** Paul Levin

**Themes:** City, Aftermath, Silence

## Synopsis: The Dead City — Paul Levin

The Dead City names a dead city. The phrase can mean ruins, a plague town, a place spiritually vacated, or a metaphor for a society. No author chooses among those.

The note will not populate the streets. Emptiness, former life, and the visitor who walks in are the elements the title itself brings.

It is a book of aftermath, whatever the scale.
