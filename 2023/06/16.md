**Themes:** Marriage, Attraction, Chemistry

## Synopsis: Elective Affinities — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

Elective Affinities takes a chemical idea, that substances leave old bonds for stronger ones, and tries it on two couples. A settled marriage invites two new people onto the estate, and affection rearranges itself with the calm of a natural law.

Goethe watches the damage without raising his voice. A child, a drowning, and a renunciation carry the moral weight.

It is a novel about desire that believes it is being rational.
