**Date:** 2023-06-14

**Title:** Reflexiones

**Author:** Marco Aurelio Antonio

**Themes:** Stoicism, Duty, Death

## Synopsis: Reflexiones — Marco Aurelio Antonio

Reflexiones is the private meditations of Marcus Aurelius in this edition's language. An emperor writes notes to his own conduct: mornings, annoyances, the nearness of death, the duty in front of him.

There is no effort to found a school in public. The repetition is the practice.

People read it when they want a stern, usable calm.

**Date:** 2023-06-14

**Title:** Self-Reflections

**Author:** Marcus Aurelius Antonius

**Themes:** Stoicism, Duty, Self-command

## Synopsis: Self-Reflections — Marcus Aurelius Antonius

Self-Reflections is the private notebook of Marcus Aurelius, a Roman emperor writing reminders to himself about death, temper, and the work in front of him. These are not speeches to the public.

He returns to the same disciplines: other people will fail you, the body is on loan, and the only thing fully yours is the use you make of your judgments.

The pages are repetitive in the way practice is repetitive. That is their use.
