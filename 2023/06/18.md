**Themes:** Relationship, Choice, Bond

## Synopsis: Relaciones electivas (Elective Affinities) — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

Relaciones electivas speaks of elective relations, bonds that are chosen rather than merely inherited. No author is given, so it will not be silently identified with a more famous novel that uses a chemical metaphor.

Choice in love and in attachment is the theme the words support. A cast of four on an estate is not assumed.

The book is about how people bind themselves. The title is already a theory of that.
