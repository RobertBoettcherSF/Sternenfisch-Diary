## Synopsis: El camino del mundo — Hildegarda de Bingen

El camino del mundo takes its subject from the phrase in the title: the way of the world, under Hildegard of Bingen's name. Her work looks for an order in creation, a path through nature and the soul rather than a private plot.

Expect vision and instruction, not a novel of manners. The prose wants a reader willing to take a cosmic frame seriously.

It sits beside her medical and musical writings as part of one large attention.
