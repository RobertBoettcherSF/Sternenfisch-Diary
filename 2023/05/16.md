## Synopsis: Conocimientos curativos — Hildegarda de Bingen

Conocimientos curativos draws on Hildegard of Bingen's knowledge of remedies: plants, stones, and the conditions of a body as she recorded them in the twelfth century. The interest is practical and visionary at once.

She writes as an abbess who observed, not as a modern clinician. The categories are her own.

The book belongs with medieval medicine and with Hildegard's larger picture of a created world that can be tended.
