## Synopsis: Collected Stories — Stefan Zweig

Collected Stories gathers Stefan Zweig's stories, the form in which he did much of his best work. A life is often caught at the moment it loses proportion: a passion, a disgrace, a confession on a journey.

The moonlight-alley subtitle on one edition points to that nocturnal, urban mood. Other pieces range wider, but the unit is the intense short tale.

It is a reading book, one story at a sitting, rather than a single arc.

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Music, Art, Essay

## Synopsis: Short texts about musicians and visual artists — Stefan Zweig

Short texts about musicians and visual artists collects Zweig's short texts on musicians and visual artists. He writes as a passionate listener and looker, tying the work to the life without pretending to be a cataloger.

The essays are invitations. A composer or a painter is approached through the pressure that made the art.

They sit beside his literary portraits as part of one lifelong attention to makers.
