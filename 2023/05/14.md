## Ambiente e mondo interno degli animali

**Date:** 2023-05-14

**Title:** Ambiente e mondo interno degli animali

**Author:** Jakob von Uexküll

**Themes:** Animals, Senses, World

## Synopsis: Ambiente e mondo interno degli animali — Jakob von Uexküll

Ambiente e mondo interno degli animali is Uexküll's account of animal worlds, in the language of this edition. Each creature perceives a different set of signs. The environment is not a single stage on which every species is an actor with the same script.

The idea of the Umwelt, a surrounding world of meaning, is the book's gift to later thought.

It changes how a walk looks. The dog, the bird, and the insect are not in your picture of the street. They are in theirs.

## Chess novella

**Date:** 2023-05-14

**Title:** Chess novella

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Chess, Captivity, Obsession

## Synopsis: Chess novella — Stefan Zweig

Chess novella is Stefan Zweig's novella about a game that becomes a prison. On an ocean liner, a crude world champion meets a stranger whose only training was a chess book memorized in solitary confinement.

The match is short. The real story is how a mind survives when the only opponent left is itself, and what happens when that practice is dragged back into daylight.

Zweig wrote it late, and the cruelty of confinement is the point, not the opening theory.

## Orgueil et Préjugés

**Date:** 2023-05-14

**Title:** Orgueil et Préjugés

**Author:** Jane Austen

**Themes:** Marriage, Pride, Manners

## Synopsis: Orgueil et Préjugés — Jane Austen

Orgueil et Préjugés is Jane Austen's novel of Elizabeth Bennet and Mr. Darcy, in the language of this edition. A family of daughters must marry, a proud man must be reread, and a witty woman must discover that her first accuracy was incomplete.

Money, embarrassment, and a great house do the structural work. The comedy is the correction of a confident mistake.

The story does not depend on the English title to be itself. The characters survive the crossing.

## The Metamorphosis

**Date:** 2023-05-14

**Title:** The Metamorphosis

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Transformation, Family, Work

## Synopsis: The Metamorphosis — Franz Kafka

The Metamorphosis begins when Gregor Samsa wakes as a monstrous insect and worries first about missing the train. The story stays inside the apartment: a job that owned him, a family that depended on his salary, and the body that ends that arrangement.

Care turns to disgust, and disgust to a practical decision. Kafka refuses a moral that would make the change a simple allegory.

What remains is the comedy and the dread of a household adjusting to someone it no longer wants to feed.
