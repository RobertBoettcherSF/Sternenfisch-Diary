**Themes:** Parable, Faith, Story

## Synopsis: Stories of the dear God — Rainer Maria Rilke

Stories of the dear God collects Rilke's Stories of the Dear God, tales told as if to explain God to a listener who might be a child or a skeptic. Neighbors, artists, and small events carry the theology.

The manner is gentle and odd. A story may end by shifting who has been speaking.

They are among his most approachable pages, and they are not simple.
