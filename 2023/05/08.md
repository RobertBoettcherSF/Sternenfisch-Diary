# 2023-05-08 — Anderswo and August Rodin

**Date:** 2023-05-08

**Title:** Anderswo

**Author:** Kurt Tucholsky

**Themes:** Sketch, Place, Wit

## Synopsis: Anderswo — Kurt Tucholsky

Anderswo takes the German word for elsewhere as its title. Tucholsky's short prose often works that way: a scene, a type, a small injustice, caught from a seat that is not quite at home.

Some editions add that these are little stories. They are glances, not epics.

The pleasure is the voice, quick and personally responsible for its jokes.

**Date:** 2023-05-08

**Title:** August Rodin

**Author:** Rainer Maria Rilke

**Themes:** Sculpture, Work, Attention

## Synopsis: August Rodin — Rainer Maria Rilke

August Rodin is Rainer Maria Rilke's writing on Auguste Rodin. He watches a sculptor whose whole method is work, patience, and the surface of a body understood as a life.

The essay is also a young poet finding a discipline. Rodin's studio becomes a lesson in staying with the thing made.

It is criticism that wants to be an apprenticeship.
