**Title:** Barthli the Korber

**Author:** Jeremias Gotthelf

**Themes:** Craft, Humility, Basket

## Synopsis: Barthli the Korber — Jeremias Gotthelf

Barthli the Korber is the story of Barthli the basket-maker, a small craftsman whose uprightness is the whole sermon and the whole plot. Gotthelf builds a life out of a trade other people overlook.

Poverty is not prettified. Neither is the quiet authority of someone who does one thing honestly.

It is a short novel of character, Swiss and concrete.

**Date:** 2023-05-11

**Title:** De wereld van gisteren

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Memory, Europe, Exile

## Synopsis: De wereld van gisteren — Stefan Zweig

De wereld van gisteren is Stefan Zweig's memoir of the Europe he lost, under this edition's title. The early pages are a grateful inventory of a cultured life. The later pages are the inventory's destruction.

War, dictatorship, and statelessness arrive as personal facts, not only as headlines. He writes from after the end.

The book is a farewell to a way of being a European, offered by someone who could not keep it.
