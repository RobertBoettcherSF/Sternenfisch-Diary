# 2023-05-18 — El camino del mundo, Healing knowledge, and The Way of the World

## El camino del mundo (The Way of the World)

**Date:** 2023-05-18

**Title:** El camino del mundo (The Way of the World)

**Author:** Hildegarda de Bingen

**Language:** Spanish

**Themes:** World, Order, Creation

## Synopsis: El camino del mundo (The Way of the World) — Hildegarda de Bingen

El camino del mundo takes its subject from the phrase in the title: the way of the world, under Hildegard of Bingen's name. Her work looks for an order in creation, a path through nature and the soul rather than a private plot.

Expect vision and instruction, not a novel of manners. The prose wants a reader willing to take a cosmic frame seriously.

It sits beside her medical and musical writings as part of one large attention.

## Healing knowledge

**Date:** 2023-05-18

**Title:** Healing knowledge

**Author:** Hildegard of Bingen

**Themes:** Healing, Plants, Body

## Synopsis: Healing knowledge — Hildegard of Bingen

Healing knowledge draws on Hildegard of Bingen's knowledge of remedies: plants, stones, and the conditions of a body as she recorded them in the twelfth century. The interest is practical and visionary at once.

She writes as an abbess who observed, not as a modern clinician. The categories are her own.

The book belongs with medieval medicine and with Hildegard's larger picture of a created world that can be tended.
