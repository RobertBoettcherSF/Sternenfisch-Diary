**Date:** 2023-05-09

**Title:** ... quite different - Picking your own bread makes you fat

**Author:** Kurt Tucholsky

**Themes:** Appetite, Proverb, Comedy

## Synopsis: ... quite different - Picking your own bread makes you fat — Kurt Tucholsky

... quite different - Picking your own bread makes you fat turns a homely warning into a book: pick your own bread and you will eat too much of it. The joke is about abundance in the hand and the loss of measure.

No author is named, and no household will be invented around the loaf. The proverb is the idea a reader can trust before opening the pages.

It sounds like a comic moral tale. The title is already laughing.

**Date:** 2023-05-09

**Title:** Of Art-Things

**Author:** Rainer Maria Rilke

**Themes:** Art, Essays, Looking

## Synopsis: Of Art-Things — Rainer Maria Rilke

Of Art-Things gathers Rilke's writings on art and on the things art attends to. The pieces are less a system than a series of visits: a picture, a place, a maker, described until the looking becomes an ethic.

He trusts attention more than opinion. That trust is the argument.

The book suits readers who want criticism slow enough to resemble prayer or work.

**Date:** 2023-05-09

**Title:** Panther, Tiger and others

**Author:** Kurt Tucholsky

**Themes:** Animals, Satire, Fable

## Synopsis: Panther, Tiger and others — Kurt Tucholsky

Panther, Tiger and others lines up a panther, a tiger, and others, which in Tucholsky's hands is a license for fable and joke rather than for natural history. Beasts let him say things about people at an angle.

The pieces are brief. A moral, if it appears, will probably grin.

Children may enjoy the animals; the bite is for adults.

**Date:** 2023-05-09

**Title:** Stories from the Murkelei

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Childhood, Region, Tale

## Synopsis: Stories from the Murkelei — Hans Fallada

Stories from the Murkelei is Hans Fallada's stories from the Murkelei, a country of his own naming, closer to memory and fable than to the Berlin of his big novels. The pieces are lighter, local, and odd.

He still notices how people actually behave. The softness is not emptiness.

It is a book of tales from a made place, meant to be visited one story at a time.
