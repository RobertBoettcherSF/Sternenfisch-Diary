## Synopsis: The Stories — Franz Kafka

The Stories is a gathering of Franz Kafka's short fiction rather than a single plot. The pieces range from parables of a few lines to stories in which an ordinary errand opens onto a procedure with no exit.

Animals, officials, sons, and messengers recur. The tone can be comic for a page and then refuse to release the joke.

A reader new to Kafka can start here and meet the method without committing to one of the unfinished novels.
