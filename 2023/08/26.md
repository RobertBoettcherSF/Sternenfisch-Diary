## Synopsis: Antigone — Sophocles of Colonos

Antigone is Sophocles' tragedy of a sister who buries a brother the city has forbidden anyone to bury. Antigone chooses the older duty, to the dead and to the gods, against Creon's edict.

The argument is not settled by a speech. A ruler who confuses the state with himself, and a girl who will not be talked out of a grave, destroy each other.

It remains a play about what a person owes the law when the law refuses the dead.
