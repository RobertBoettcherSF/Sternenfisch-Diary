## Synopsis: Antigona: Una historia de desafío y destino — Sófocles de Colono

Antigona: Una historia de desafío y destino is Sophocles' Antigone in the language of this edition. A sister buries a brother against a ruler's edict and accepts the consequence rather than the city's version of piety.

Creon's certainty is as dangerous as her resolve. The play does not offer a comfortable compromise.

It is a short tragedy about the dead and about the living who think they own the law.
