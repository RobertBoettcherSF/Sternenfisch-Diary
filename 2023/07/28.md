**Author:** Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

**Themes:** Botany, Form, Metamorphosis

## Synopsis: Metamorfosis de las plantas — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

Metamorfosis de las plantas is Goethe's metamorphosis of plants in Spanish. He proposes that the organs of a plant are variations of one form, seen changing through the plant's life.

The essay trains the eye. It is science written by a poet who distrusted mere lists.

Readers of his fiction will recognize the same interest in transformation.
