## Synopsis: Wilhelm Meister's Apprenticeship — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

Wilhelm Meister's Apprenticeship follows Wilhelm Meister as he leaves a merchant life for the theater, convinced that acting will make him the person he hopes to be. Guilds of actors, a mysterious child, and a secret society interrupt that plan.

Goethe turns a young man's self-cultivation into a tour of eighteenth-century callings. The theater is both a true education and a beautiful mistake.

The novel is long, sociable, and interested in how a life finds work that is not only a romance.
