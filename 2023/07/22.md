## Synopsis: Metamorphosis of plants: Natural Science Essay — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

Metamorphosis of plants: Natural Science Essay is Goethe's essay on the metamorphosis of plants. He looks for one leaf-form changing itself through the life of a plant, from cotyledon to flower, instead of treating each organ as a separate invention.

The prose is observant and speculative. He wants a way of seeing, not a catalog of species.

Gardeners and readers of his novels both find the same habit here: watching a form transform without breaking.
