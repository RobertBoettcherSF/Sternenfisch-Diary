## Synopsis: Los años de aprendizaje de Wilhelm Meister — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

Los años de aprendizaje de Wilhelm Meister is the Spanish edition of Goethe's Wilhelm Meister's apprenticeship. A merchant's son seeks an education in the theater and finds that a life is shaped by more teachers than he chose.

Actors, a strange child, and a hidden society redirect him. The book believes in development and is honest about detours.

It is a long novel of self-formation.
