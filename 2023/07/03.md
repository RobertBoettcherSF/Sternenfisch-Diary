## Synopsis: Wilhelm Meister's Years of Travel — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

Wilhelm Meister's Years of Travel is the later Wilhelm Meister book, in which apprenticeship gives way to travel, craft, and a more severe idea of usefulness. The wandering is less about becoming a genius and more about finding a limited, real task.

Stories told along the way open onto education, art, and the management of desire. Goethe is older here, and more interested in renunciation than in expansion.

It helps to have met Wilhelm in the apprenticeship novel, but the mood is distinct: a life narrowing toward service.
