## Synopsis: Los años de viaje de Wilhelm Meister (Wilhelm Meister's Years of Travel) — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

Los años de viaje de Wilhelm Meister is Wilhelm Meister's years of travel, the later Goethe novel in which wandering replaces the dream of being a great actor. Usefulness, craft, and a tighter self take the stage.

Inset stories carry much of the thinking. The mood is of a man learning what to give up.

It continues the apprenticeship on sterner terms.
