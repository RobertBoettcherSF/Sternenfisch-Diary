## Synopsis: The Sorrows of Young Werther — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

The Sorrows of Young Werther is Goethe's novel in letters about a young man who loves Lotte and cannot fit the feeling into a life. Werther's enthusiasm for nature, children, and Ossian curdles when the beloved remains married and ordinary duty closes in.

The book made a generation's style of sorrow. It is also a precise picture of a self that feeds on its own intensity.

Read now, it is both a love story and a warning about a sensibility with nowhere to go.
