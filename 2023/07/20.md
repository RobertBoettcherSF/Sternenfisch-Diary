## Synopsis: Las penas del joven Werther (The Sorrows of Young Werther) — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

Las penas del joven Werther is Goethe's Sorrows of Young Werther in Spanish. A young man's letters trace a love for Lotte that becomes his whole climate, and then a trap.

Nature, reading, and a rival who is simply her husband fill the pages. The sorrow is eloquent and, by the end, lethal.

The novel made a fashion of feeling, and it also diagnoses that fashion.
