## El rabino de Bacherach

**Date:** 2023-01-06

**Title:** El rabino de Bacherach

**Author:** Heinrich Heine

**Themes:** Faith, Persecution, Journey

## Synopsis: El rabino de Bacherach — Heinrich Heine

El rabino de Bacherach is Heinrich Heine's unfinished tale of a rabbi of Bacherach who, at Passover, discovers a persecution in time to flee with his wife. The feast, the river, and the city they reach are rendered with a tenderness Heine does not always allow his subjects.

The manuscript breaks off. What exists is already a story about danger inside a religious life, and about a poet of Jewish origin writing that danger in German.

It is short, beautiful, and deliberately incomplete.

## Pensamientos e ideas

**Date:** 2023-01-06

**Title:** Pensamientos e ideas

**Author:** Heinrich Heine

**Themes:** Wit, Exile, Ideas

## Synopsis: Pensamientos e ideas — Heinrich Heine

Pensamientos e ideas gathers Heinrich Heine's thoughts and ideas, the prose of a poet who used jokes as a serious instrument. Exile, faith, Germany, and the theater of modern opinion pass through short forms.

He is rarely solemn for a whole page. The lightness is a tactic, not a lack of stake.

The Spanish edition under a twin title is the same kind of book: reflections, not a novel.
