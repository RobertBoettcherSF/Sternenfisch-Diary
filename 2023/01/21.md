## Synopsis: Collected Works — Heinrich Heine

Collected Works is a wide gathering of Heinrich Heine, poet of love songs and of political bite, prose satirist, and exile. No single plot can stand for a collected works.

A reader should expect lyrics that feel simple and then turn, and prose that treats Europe's arguments as comedy with a wound under it.

The edition is a library of one voice, not one story. The voice is the reason to open it.
