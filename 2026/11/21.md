**Date:** 2026-11-21

**Title:** bardo-visualization

**Tags:** ada, wasm

## Note

bardo-visualization is an interactive tour of the Bardo states. The clock, the curves, and the mantra syllables are plain Ada, compiled to WebAssembly. The page draws, and the words on it are the project's own.

The default order is the book's. It starts at the primary Clear Light, the moment of death, and runs forward to rebirth. Jung's reverse order is the other choice on the order toggle, and the page remembers that choice. Follow moves toward liberation. The other control moves toward rebirth. A day scrubber jumps to the screen for that day.
