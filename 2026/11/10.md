## Aforismos sobre la sabiduría de la vida: Lo que uno es, lo que uno tiene y lo que uno representa (Aphorisms on the Wisdom of Life)

**Date:** 2026-11-10

**Title:** Aforismos sobre la sabiduría de la vida: Lo que uno es, lo que uno tiene y lo que uno representa (Aphorisms on the Wisdom of Life)

**Author:** Arthur Schopenhauer

**Language:** Spanish

**Themes:** Character, Property, Reputation

## Synopsis: Aforismos sobre la sabiduría de la vida: Lo que uno es, lo que uno tiene y lo que uno representa (Aphorisms on the Wisdom of Life) — Arthur Schopenhauer

Aforismos sobre la sabiduría de la vida is Schopenhauer's practical essay on what a life can actually hold. He sorts the goods of life into three, and the subtitle names them: what a person is, in character, health, and temperament; what a person has, in property; and what a person represents, in rank, honor, and the opinion of others.

The ranking is blunt. What one is outweighs the other two, because pleasure and pain are felt from the inside. Property can ease a life. Reputation is the most fragile of the three, since it lives in other people's heads and leaves when they do.

The chapters are counsel rather than a system of metaphysics. A reader comes for a clear order of the things people chase, and for the advice that follows from putting character first.

## El ocaso de los ídolos (Traducido): o cómo se filosofa con el martillo (Twilight of the Idols: or How to Philosophize with a Hammer)

**Date:** 2026-11-10

**Title:** El ocaso de los ídolos (Traducido): o cómo se filosofa con el martillo (Twilight of the Idols: or How to Philosophize with a Hammer)

**Author:** Friedrich Nietzsche

**Language:** Spanish

**Themes:** Idols, Critique, Style

## Synopsis: El ocaso de los ídolos (Traducido): o cómo se filosofa con el martillo (Twilight of the Idols: or How to Philosophize with a Hammer) — Friedrich Nietzsche

El ocaso de los ídolos is Nietzsche in a hurry, knocking down philosophers, moralists, and the pieties he regarded as idols. The sections are brief, often a page, and written to be remembered as blows.

Socrates, morality treated as anti-nature, and the habit of confusing a cause with a consequence are among the targets. The hammer of the subtitle is a tuning hammer: he sounds the idols to hear which ones are hollow.

It is a handbook of his late opinions, faster and sharper than the earlier aphorism books. A reader who wants the late Nietzsche in short strikes can start here.

## Hood-Simulator

Hood Simulator is a Prohibition-era crime strategy game. You plan the gang's week at the desk, then watch it play out on the streets of a fully 3D city. A later extension, still named as a plan, would carry the same mechanics from Saint Volstead in 1932 to an imaginary city in 2032.

The browser build is the play page. Saves in the browser stay in that browser.

---

## Megacorp-Simulator

Megacorp Simulator is a campaign of generated cities, written in Ada 2012. The browser page draws what the Ada module computes. Version 0.28.0 offers Harbour, Foundry, and Marches as locked worlds, and free play takes a typed seed or a rolled one.

On the map you open a region, then walk and fight the mission. Pause and the speed controls advance the campaign. A save stays in the browser, and Continue restores it. The live page is the current `master` build.

---

## ADA-Megacorp-Simulator

ADA-Megacorp-Simulator is a headless simulation written in Ada, with a thin browser page that draws what the Ada program has already exported. The public build is version 0.8.0. The check page is `web/index.html` on `master`.

The code, the tools, the documents, and the placeholder art written for the project are MIT.
