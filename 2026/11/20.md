## ada-density-explorer

ada-density-explorer is a page for learning physical density by moving sliders and watching the picture move with the numbers. Every calculation is plain Ada, compiled to WebAssembly. HTML and CSS draw the page. JavaScript loads the module, wires the controls, and paints the figures.

The stops run from a float tank and a packed box, through equal volumes on a balance, to a density ladder from interstellar space to a neutron star and a zoom from a solid object toward a proton. A last section is labelled as a thought experiment, separate from the measurements.

---

## ada-fractal-explorer

ada-fractal-explorer is a Mandelbrot explorer whose mathematics is plain Ada, compiled to WebAssembly. The browser blits the pixels and handles the pointer, the menus, the URL, and saving a PNG. This first slice is the Mandelbrot set. Named places include Seahorse Valley, Elephant Valley, and the largest mini-brot on the spike.

Colour is how long an orbit took to escape. The iteration limit rises as you zoom, until Long_Float runs out of fraction bits and neighbouring pixels become the same number. The page says so when the picture breaks into blocks.
