# 2026-11-22 — ADA-Hood-Simulator

**Date:** 2026-11-22

**Title:** ADA-Hood-Simulator

**Tags:** ada, wasm, game

## Note

ADA-Hood-Simulator is a browser strategy game set in Saint Volstead in 1932. You run a gang, take the city block by block, and outlast the rival gangs. The simulation — city, people, orders, movement, combat, police, economy, rival gangs, and the autoplayer — is plain Ada 2022 with contracts, compiled to WebAssembly. TypeScript and Three.js draw the street, the panels, and the sound, and they read the Ada core through an integer-only interface.

The public build has reached the city, the people walking it, the week clock with wages and loyalty, and the peaceful orders with their crew commands. The behaviour it is measured against is the public Hood-Simulator tree.
