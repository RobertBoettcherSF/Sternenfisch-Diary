## holographic-principle

holographic-principle is a child-friendly guided tour of the holographic principle. Each card opens with one plain sentence and a picture you can move. The fold "Show me the maths" holds the formula, a worked sample, and a live recalculation.

Schwarzschild radius, Bekenstein–Hawking entropy, sugar-cube counts, the Poincaré disk, geodesics, and the HaPPY code are Ada compiled to WebAssembly. JavaScript draws, runs the menus, and writes the feedback file. GitHub Pages serves the tour, in UK English, German, Spanish, and French.

---

## godel-escher-bach

godel-escher-bach is a browser companion to the themes of Gödel, Escher, Bach, one chapter at a time. The words and the pictures are original. The open chapters include the MIU blocks, a runner who closes half the remaining gap, figure and ground turning into birds, nested frames and rabbits, a crab canon read forward and backward, and an ant fugue.

The rules for each of those are Ada compiled to WebAssembly. JavaScript only draws. GitHub Pages serves the gallery, in UK English, German, Spanish, and French. Further chapters are drawn as closed tiles.
