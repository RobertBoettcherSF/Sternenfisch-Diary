**Tags:** ada, wasm

## Note

ada-technological-horizons is an independent interactive exploration inspired by Stanisław Lem's Summa Technologiae. The page is original educational software: book chapters opened as sections, a catalogue, and calculators whose arithmetic runs in Ada compiled to WebAssembly. Shell text is in UK English, German, Spanish, and French.

The writing on the page is its own. It acknowledges the book as a literary inspiration and does not reproduce it. The static tree in `dist/` is the page a browser opens.
