## Synopsis: Aurora (Traducido): Pensamientos sobre los prejuicios morales (Dawn: Thoughts on Moral Prejudices) — Friedrich Nietzsche

Aurora is Nietzsche's book of aphorisms on the moral feelings people inherit and rarely inspect. Custom, pity, punishment, and the sting of guilt are treated as prejudices with a history.

The tone is cooler than the later polemics. He is trying to slow the reflex that calls a habit a duty. The free spirit of these pages is someone willing to live for a while without a ready moral verdict.

The book sits between Human, All Too Human and The Gay Science. It is the morning work: a long, patient taking-apart of the prejudices that make a conscience feel natural.

**Author:** Hildegarda de Bingen (Hildegard of Bingen)

**Language:** Portuguese

**Themes:** Healing, Plants, Body

## Synopsis: Conhecimentos curativos (Traducido): Os escritos da abadessa Hildegarda sobre as causas e o tratamento das doenças (Healing Knowledge) — Hildegarda de Bingen (Hildegard of Bingen)

Conhecimentos curativos draws on Hildegard of Bingen's knowledge of remedies: plants, stones, and the conditions of a body as she recorded them in the twelfth century. The interest is practical and visionary at once.

She writes as an abbess who observed, not as a modern clinician. The categories are her own.

The book belongs with medieval medicine and with Hildegard's larger picture of a created world that can be tended.
