## interactive-formulas

interactive-formulas is a learning lab for formulas. The page introduces itself as Syllabus-Repo. A card shows the relation, what the symbols mean, sample inputs, a result, and the substituted steps. Chemistry, mathematics, physics, and computer science each open as a gallery of those cards, in UK English, German, Spanish, and French.

GitHub Pages serves the site. The introduction keeps an embedded WebAssembly module. The cards themselves are learning aids: displayed results are rounded, and the page says they are not tools for safety-critical work.

---

## core-flight-ada

Core Flight Ada is an independent Ada analogue of a cFS-style flight executive. A cyclic tick, a software bus, events, time, tables, and a sample application sit in one tree, with a browser splash and console and a native Linux program.

JavaScript draws the frames and handles the menus. The flight loop and the splash math run in Ada compiled to WebAssembly. The core packages enable SPARK. The write-up of what is proved and what is not is in the repository, and it is not offered as a certification.

---

## eva-planner-ada

EVA Planner Ada is an independent Ada analogue of exploration planning: one EVA, two walkers, stations and points of interest on an invented south-pole-like map, with walk times that include slope, walkback limits, and science coverage.

The splash and the console run in the browser. JavaScript lays out the tables. The map and the walkers are Ada compiled to WebAssembly, and the table numbers match the native program. The site and the heights are invented for this first slice.
