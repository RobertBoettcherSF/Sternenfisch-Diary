**Date:** 2026-11-09

**Title:** Ada-Ark-Two-Simulator

**Tags:** ada, wasm

## Note

Ada-Ark-Two-Simulator is a browser walk-through of Ark Two, the community fallout shelter Bruce M. Beach built in Ontario, shown in an ideal working state and made in his memory. A glTF model exported from Blender is viewed with three.js. The simulation logic is Ada, aimed at WebAssembly. Rooms, suites A–G, and archive entries sit in JSON beside the model.

On a computer, click and then walk with the keys and the mouse. On a phone, a thumb stick moves and a drag looks. The models, the code, and the data in the repository are the project's own.
