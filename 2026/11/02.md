## Synopsis: Faust — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

Faust is the drama of the scholar who has exhausted books and makes a bargain with the devil for a fuller life. Goethe's play follows Faust from the study to love, politics, and a last enormous project of land reclaimed from the sea.

Gretchen's story is the human cost of his hunger. Mephistopheles supplies wit, means, and a running mockery of the desire he serves.

The work asks whether striving itself can be a kind of salvation, and it does not make the answer comfortable.

## Synopsis: Fausto (Faust) — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

Fausto is the Faust drama: a scholar who bargains with the devil because books have ceased to be a life. Love, power, and a last attempt to build something that justifies the bargain carry the action.

Gretchen is the human price. The devil is intelligent company and a prosecutor.

Under the German or the Romance title, it is the same huge question about striving.
