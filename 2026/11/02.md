## Faust: The First Part of the Tragedy

**Date:** 2026-11-02

**Title:** Faust: The First Part of the Tragedy

**Author:** Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

**Themes:** Ambition, Bargain, Love

## Synopsis: Faust: The First Part of the Tragedy — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

Faust: The First Part of the Tragedy opens on a scholar who has finished the books and found them empty. In his study he turns toward magic, and Mephistopheles offers him the world on one condition: the moment Faust asks time to stay, the bargain is forfeit.

The play follows him into the street, the tavern, and the love of Margarete, called Gretchen. Her mother, her brother, and her child are the price of his rejuvenated appetite. The devil supplies wit, gifts, and a way out of every room except the last.

The first part ends in a prison. Gretchen refuses the escape Faust brings her. The question left standing is whether a hunger for experience can be told apart from the harm it does.

**Language:** Spanish

## Synopsis: Fausto: Primera parte de la tragedia (Faust: The First Part of the Tragedy) — Johann Wolfgang von Goethe

Fausto: Primera parte de la tragedia is the first part of Goethe's tragedy: a scholar who bargains with the devil because books have ceased to be a life. The condition is exact. If Faust ever asks one moment to stay, he is lost.

Love carries the human cost. Gretchen's mother, her brother, and her child stand between his renewed appetite and any claim that the bargain was only a private experiment. Mephistopheles is intelligent company and the means of every shortcut.

The first part closes in a prison. Gretchen refuses the escape that is offered. Striving and harm are left in the same room.
