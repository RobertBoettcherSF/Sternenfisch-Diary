## Synopsis: Pinocchio – Historien om den trefiguren Bengele

Pinocchio – Historien om den trefiguren Bengele names Pinocchio, the wooden figure who wants a life a puppet is not supposed to have. The tale runs on temptation: a lie that grows, a playground that eats boys, and a father who goes looking anyway.

The conscience in the story is often a voice the puppet would rather not hear. Becoming real is treated as a moral labor, not as a costume change.

The edition's title points at that wooden child. The note stays with the story those names already carry.

**Title:** Verden av i går

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Memory, Europe, Exile

## Synopsis: Verden av i går — Stefan Zweig

Verden av i går is Stefan Zweig's memoir of the Europe he lost, under this edition's title. The early pages are a grateful inventory of a cultured life. The later pages are the inventory's destruction.

War, dictatorship, and statelessness arrive as personal facts, not only as headlines. He writes from after the end.

The book is a farewell to a way of being a European, offered by someone who could not keep it.
