## Synopsis: Castellio against Calvin — Stefan Zweig

Castellio against Calvin sets Sebastian Castellio against John Calvin after the burning of Michael Servetus. Zweig takes the side of the man who argued that killing a heretic does not defend the truth.

Geneva's discipline, the pamphlets, and the loneliness of the dissenter are the scenery. The book is a polemic in the form of history.

Its question is simple and still uncomfortable: who has the right to punish a belief.

**Title:** Three masters

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Writers, Biography, Craft

## Synopsis: Three masters — Stefan Zweig

Three masters is Stefan Zweig's set of portraits of Balzac, Dickens, and Dostoevsky. He treats each novelist as a kind of excess: the builder of a world, the celebrant of ordinary hearts, the examiner of guilt.

The essays are not plot summaries. They look for the pressure that made the work unavoidable.

Read together, they are a small map of the nineteenth-century novel as three temperaments.
