## Synopsis: पिता को पत्र — Franz Kafka

पिता को पत्र is Hindi for a letter to a father. The form is an address from a child to a parent, often the place where love and judgment finally get said.

No author's name is attached here, so this note will not borrow another writer's famous letter and call it this one. The situation in the title is enough.

A reader should expect a voice speaking upward, toward the person who had the first authority.
