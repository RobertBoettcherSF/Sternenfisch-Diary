Ladestöcke in der Nacht is Marian too, and it has its own section: the description is a full scene, a tactical aesthetic together with Marian honor. A hip-hop song generated with Suno, 3:28. The description states the aim in one sentence: express that Gottesmutter Maria, Godmother Mary, should ask that the dead may be allowed into heaven.
