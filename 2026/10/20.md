## Note

Diary Sim is published as a public Ada 2022 repository. Four beings (the wesen) write a diary line per tick, teach and coin words, and grow a shared vocabulary. Each run replays its saved history from the seed and checks it byte for byte. `make test` runs the unit tests and a smoke test; GitHub Actions builds with GNAT 12 and 14.

## माँ मरियम का आंचल

The same day I uploaded a Hindi Marian song to Robert Fieldnotes. Suno generated it. The title, माँ मरियम का आंचल, is Mary's mantle, her veil. The description is that title and a Hindi call to action. The track runs 3:39.

It belongs to the Marian batch on the channel: praise of Godmother Mary, here in Hindi, on the day the Ada diary simulation went public.
