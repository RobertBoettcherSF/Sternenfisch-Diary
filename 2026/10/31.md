## Synopsis: Par-delà le bien et le mal — Friedrich Nietzsche

Par-delà le bien et le mal is Beyond Good and Evil in the language of this edition. Nietzsche treats moral judgments as symptoms and asks what kind of life each virtue protects.

The chapters are sequences of strikes, not a gentle system. A reader is expected to be able to lose an argument with the page.

It is a central book of his mature thought.
