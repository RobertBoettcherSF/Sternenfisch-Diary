## Note

Three more Suno experiments on Robert Fieldnotes. Two belong to the EDM batch: tracks named for Hindu ritual terms I find meaningful. The third is a Marian Short in French.

## Abhisheka

Abhisheka is the ritual anointing, the pouring. The track runs 4:57. The description is only the Hindi thank-you and the ask to like, share, and subscribe: वीडियो देखने के लिए धन्यवाद! कृपया लाइक, शेयर और सब्सक्राइब करें। The title carries the term. I treat it as part of that Suno EDM batch.

## Arti

Arti, also spelled aarti, is the lamp-offering ritual. The track runs 5:02. The description includes a Hindi thank-you and the hashtag #अल्टरिंगकीमैरी. That hashtag ties the title to the Altötting Mary theme on the channel. Same batch: Suno EDM named for a Hindu term I care about, with that Marian tag on the description.

## Marie, Reste Avec Nous

A Short, 2:02. The French title asks Mary to stay with us. The description is the hashtag #rapcatholique. Marian praise, Catholic rap, generated with Suno.
