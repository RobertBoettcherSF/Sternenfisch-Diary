## Make Sure Nothing Breaks

The essay "Make Sure Nothing Breaks: Rebuilding the Foundations of Machine Intelligence" is published as Alpha, under CC BY 4.0. It is a short public note on efficiency, and on wasting less work to crashes, silent errors, and thrown-away runs. The text says it is not an academic paper and not a claim about machine consciousness. Comments are welcome as GitHub issues.

## UI mockups

The first public mockups are plain HTML and CSS: a farm weather console, a drone console, and the VerdantOS and AetherOS desktops. The gallery is standalone HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with a small Ada core compiled to WebAssembly so language and region follow the flag (German, English, Spanish, and French). The live gallery is on GitHub Pages.
