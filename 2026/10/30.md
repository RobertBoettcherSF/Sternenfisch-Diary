# 2026-10-30 — Four Marian Suno experiments

**Date:** 2026-10-30

**Title:** Four Marian Suno experiments

**Tags:** youtube, fieldnotes, suno, marian

## Note

Four experiments on Robert Fieldnotes, all in the Marian batch. Suno generated them. Two are German hip-hop joined to Marienverehrung, veneration of Mary. Two are in English: one asks Godmother Mary to protect us, and one is EDM that asks her to protect sinners.

## Mother Mary, Cover Us

3:32. An experiment with Suno about asking Godmother Mary to protect us. The title asks her to cover us.

## Maria, steh uns bei

3:24. An experiment in connecting German hip-hop, through Suno, with Marian veneration. The title asks her to stand by us: Maria, steh uns bei.

## Maria, hör uns zu

3:02. An experiment with Suno that connects German hip-hop with that same veneration. The title asks her to listen: Maria, hör uns zu.

The lyric excerpt is worth keeping in full. It is the request in plain lines, ellipses and all:

> Maria, hör uns zu. Wir kommen mit den Händen voller Fragen… Maria, schütz die Sünder, schütz die Müden auf dem Weg… KI kann Muster zeigen… Doch nur wir können an der Schwelle bleiben… Maria, segne Hände, die die Technik menschlich lenken…

In English, that excerpt says: Mary, listen to us. We come with our hands full of questions. Mary, protect the sinners, protect the weary on the way. AI can show patterns. Only we can remain at the threshold. Mary, bless the hands that guide the technology in a human way.

The lines about patterns and about hands are the ones that match how these tracks were made. Suno can show a pattern. The plea in the song is still for human hands, and for Mary over the sinners and the tired.

## Mary, Keep the Door

5:22. An experiment in making EDM that asks Godmother Mary to protect sinners. The title asks her to keep the door.
