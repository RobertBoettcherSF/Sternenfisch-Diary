# 2026-10-26 — Symmetry visualizations

**Date:** 2026-10-26

**Title:** Symmetry visualizations

**Tags:** ada, wasm

## Note

Sixteen animated visualizations in plain Ada, compiled to WebAssembly, for Ada students who want to program something beautiful. The set includes Rule 30, the Game of Life, field lines, a growth tree, the Lorenz attractor, and a few fractals. One file, `dist/index.html`, holds the module and a thin drawing layer. The page cycles the pictures, and Space steps to the next one.
