## Synopsis: Nicomachean Ethics — Aristoteles of Stagira

Nicomachean Ethics is Aristotle's inquiry into how a human life goes well. Happiness, for him, is an activity of the soul in accordance with virtue, built by habit rather than by a single heroic mood.

Friendship, pleasure, justice, and the mean between excess and defect are worked through with examples from a city he knew. The prose is a teacher's, not a mystic's.

It remains a practical book: character is something you train, in company, over time.
