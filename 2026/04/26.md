## Synopsis: Petit Homme — Hans Fallada

Petit Homme is Fallada's little man and the question of what now, in the language of this edition. A young pair try to make a family on wages that will not support a family.

The politics are in the price of food and the tone of officials. No speech is more important than the rent.

It is one of the great novels of ordinary economics.
