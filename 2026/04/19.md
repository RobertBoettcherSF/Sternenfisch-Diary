## Synopsis: Official Report of the Pastor of Eichengrün — Friedrich Rochlitz

Official Report of the Pastor of Eichengrün is an official report by a pastor, addressed to or concerning Eichengrün. The form is clerical and administrative: a shepherd of souls writing up a place.

No author is named. The charm and the seriousness of such a title are the same, a village seen by the person paid to care for its consciences.

Expect observation, complaint, and the texture of a parish, not a thriller.
