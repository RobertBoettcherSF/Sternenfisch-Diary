# 2026-02-08 — How could the world end? and La Colección Invisible

**Date:** 2026-02-08

**Title:** How could the world end?

**Themes:** Ending, Speculation, World

## Synopsis: How could the world end?

How could the world end? asks how the world could end. The question is the book. Cosmic, religious, scientific, or satirical answers are all compatible with the words, and none will be picked without evidence.

No author is named. What a reader can expect is an attempt to face finality rather than a domestic plot that happens to use a grand phrase.

The title is a speculation. The pages are the form that speculation takes.

**Date:** 2026-02-08

**Title:** La Colección Invisible

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Collection, Pride, Ruin

## Synopsis: La Colección Invisible — Stefan Zweig

La Colección Invisible is Zweig's invisible collection: an old man displays treasures that have already been sold, and everyone who loves him agrees to see them. Inflation is the economic fact. Tenderness and self-deception are the human ones.

The story is very short. It does not need to be longer.

Art, in this tale, is what a person believes is still on the wall.
