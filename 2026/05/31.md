La aventura de la Nochevieja is an adventure of New Year's night, in English or in Spanish. The last night of the year is a traditional stage for wagers, apparitions, and decisions that would look silly in March.

## The black spider

**Date:** 2026-05-31

**Title:** The black spider

**Author:** Jeremias Gotthelf

**Themes:** Village, Plague, Pact

## Synopsis: The black spider — Jeremias Gotthelf

The black spider is Jeremias Gotthelf's tale of a Swiss village that makes a bargain in a time of plague and inherits a black spider. Faith, greed, and the courage of ordinary people are tested across two generations.

The spider is not a decoration. It is the visible form of a promise that should never have been made.

The novella is famous because the horror and the village piety are equally exact.

## The fugitive in the Jura

**Date:** 2026-05-31

**Title:** The fugitive in the Jura

**Author:** Heinrich Zschokke

**Themes:** Flight, Mountains, Refuge

## Synopsis: The fugitive in the Jura — Heinrich Zschokke

The fugitive in the Jura places a fugitive in the Jura, the folded country on the French-Swiss edge, a classic landscape for hiding. Flight and terrain are the title's whole machine.

No author is named, so no historical identity will be assigned to the runner. Mountains that complicate a pursuit are the setting.

It is an adventure of escape, or a moral tale about someone who must not be found. The Jura is the constant.
