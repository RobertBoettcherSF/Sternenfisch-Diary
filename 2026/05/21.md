## Synopsis: Mente Creativa y Éxito (Creative Mind and Success) — Ernest Holmes

Mente Creativa y Éxito is Ernest Holmes writing on creative mind and success, in the New Thought line that treats consciousness as a practical power. The essays urge a disciplined use of attention, belief, and inner statement.

This note will not turn his claims into a method or a promise of results. The book is what it says it is: a series of arguments that thought shapes a life.

Readers who want that tradition will find it here without a scientific disguise.
