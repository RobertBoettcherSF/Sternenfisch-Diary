## Synopsis: Poor People — Fyodor Mikhailovich Dostoevsky

Poor People is Dostoevsky's first novel, told entirely in letters between a poor clerk and a young woman in the same Petersburg building. Makar Devushkin spends money he does not have to protect Varvara, and the letters record the dignity and the panic of that effort.

There is no detective plot. The drama is rent, a new dress, a dangerous offer from a richer man, and the way affection talks when it is ashamed.

The book asks what goodness is worth when it cannot pay the bill.
