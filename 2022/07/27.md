## Entorno y mundo interior de los animales (Environment and Inner World of Animals)

**Date:** 2022-07-27

**Title:** Entorno y mundo interior de los animales (Environment and Inner World of Animals)

**Author:** Jakob von Uexküll

**Language:** Spanish

**Themes:** Animals, Senses, World

## Synopsis: Entorno y mundo interior de los animales (Environment and Inner World of Animals) — Jakob von Uexküll

Entorno y mundo interior de los animales is Uexküll's account of animal worlds, in the language of this edition. Each creature perceives a different set of signs. The environment is not a single stage on which every species is an actor with the same script.

The idea of the Umwelt, a surrounding world of meaning, is the book's gift to later thought.

It changes how a walk looks. The dog, the bird, and the insect are not in your picture of the street. They are in theirs.

## Environment and inner world of the animals

**Date:** 2022-07-27

**Title:** Environment and inner world of the animals

**Author:** Jakob Johann von Uexküll

**Themes:** Animals, Perception, Environment

## Synopsis: Environment and inner world of the animals — Jakob Johann von Uexküll

Environment and inner world of the animals is Jakob von Uexküll's study of how animals inhabit worlds built from their own senses. A tick, a sea urchin, or a dog does not enter 'the' environment; each lives inside the cues that matter to it.

The argument changed biology and later philosophy. Meaning, for Uexküll, is what a creature can perceive and answer.

The prose is scientific and pictorial. It asks the reader to stop assuming that human sight is the measure of a place.

## Reseñas 1902 - 1939 (Reviews 1902–1939)

**Date:** 2022-07-27

**Title:** Reseñas 1902 - 1939 (Reviews 1902–1939)

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Language:** Spanish

**Themes:** Books, Criticism, Reading

## Synopsis: Reseñas 1902 - 1939 (Reviews 1902–1939) — Stefan Zweig

Reseñas 1902 - 1939 collects Stefan Zweig's reviews and literary encounters across the years named in the title. The pieces are responses to books and to the writers who made them, written as a working reader rather than as a system.

Taken together they sketch a taste: European, biographical, impatient with dullness, loyal to intensity.

It is a book for someone who likes criticism that still sounds like a person talking.

## Viaje a Rusia (Trip to Russia)

**Date:** 2022-07-27

**Title:** Viaje a Rusia (Trip to Russia)

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Language:** Spanish

**Themes:** Russia, Travel, Writers

## Synopsis: Viaje a Rusia (Trip to Russia) — Stefan Zweig

Viaje a Rusia is Zweig's account of a trip to Russia, curious, literary, and personal. He goes as a European man of letters meeting a world he has mostly known through its novels.

The value is the traveler's eye, not a political system explained. Encounters and atmospheres do the work.

It belongs with his other travel pages, before exile made travel a different kind of fact.
