## Intoxicación de la metamorfosis

**Date:** 2022-07-26

**Title:** Intoxicación de la metamorfosis

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Love, Poverty, Transformation

## Synopsis: Intoxicación de la metamorfosis — Stefan Zweig

Intoxicación de la metamorfosis is the novel published in English as The Post-Office Girl and in German as the intoxication of transformation. A young woman is briefly lifted into a life of hotels and ease, then sent back, and the return poisons what she can bear.

Zweig studies resentment as a chemical result of a glimpse. Love and crime become imaginable in the same lowered room.

The book is harsher than his nostalgic essays. It knows what a short holiday of wealth can do.

## María Antonieta

**Date:** 2022-07-26

**Title:** María Antonieta

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Queen, Revolution, Portrait

## Synopsis: María Antonieta — Stefan Zweig

María Antonieta is Zweig's Marie Antoinette under this title. A girl is sent to Versailles, a queen is hated with pleasure by a public, and a prisoner is what remains when the ceremony ends.

He looks for the woman inside the caricature. The Revolution is not acquitted, and neither is the court.

It is a biography written as a tragedy of miscasting.

## María Estuardo

**Date:** 2022-07-26

**Title:** María Estuardo

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Queens, Rivalry, History

## Synopsis: María Estuardo — Stefan Zweig

María Estuardo is Stefan Zweig's life of Mary, Queen of Scots, written against the long shadow of Elizabeth. The book follows a queen whose marriages, religion, and claim to a neighboring throne made her both romantic and dangerous.

Captivity in England occupies as much of the story as the Scottish reign. Zweig watches a political prisoner become a symbol, and a symbol become a sentence.

The subject is royal charisma under guard, and the use two kingdoms made of one woman's name.

## Reseñas 1902 - 1939

**Date:** 2022-07-26

**Title:** Reseñas 1902 - 1939

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Books, Criticism, Reading

## Synopsis: Reseñas 1902 - 1939 — Stefan Zweig

Reseñas 1902 - 1939 collects Stefan Zweig's reviews and literary encounters across the years named in the title. The pieces are responses to books and to the writers who made them, written as a working reader rather than as a system.

Taken together they sketch a taste: European, biographical, impatient with dullness, loyal to intensity.

It is a book for someone who likes criticism that still sounds like a person talking.

## Viaje a Rusia

**Date:** 2022-07-26

**Title:** Viaje a Rusia

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Russia, Travel, Writers

## Synopsis: Viaje a Rusia — Stefan Zweig

Viaje a Rusia is Zweig's account of a trip to Russia, curious, literary, and personal. He goes as a European man of letters meeting a world he has mostly known through its novels.

The value is the traveler's eye, not a political system explained. Encounters and atmospheres do the work.

It belongs with his other travel pages, before exile made travel a different kind of fact.

## Viajes por Europa

**Date:** 2022-07-26

**Title:** Viajes por Europa

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Travel, Europe, Observation

## Synopsis: Viajes por Europa — Stefan Zweig

Viajes por Europa follows Stefan Zweig's travels in Europe, the continent he treated as a single cultural home. Cities, meetings, and the pleasure of crossing borders without drama fill the pages.

The later knowledge that those borders would harden gives the journeys a shadow they did not all have at the time. The book can be read for the delight alone.

It is prose of movement, by a writer who thought civilization was a way of visiting one another.
