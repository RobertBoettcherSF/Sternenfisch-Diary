# 2022-07-20 — Amerigo, Primera experiencia, and Tres poetas, de su vida

## Amerigo

**Date:** 2022-07-20

**Title:** Amerigo

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Exploration, Name, America

## Synopsis: Amerigo — Stefan Zweig

Amerigo is Stefan Zweig's portrait of Amerigo Vespucci, the man whose first name was given to two continents. Zweig is interested in how a navigator's letters became a baptism for a world.

The drama is reputation as much as seamanship. A name sticks, and the sticking is a historical event of its own.

It is a short biographical essay in Zweig's swift manner, about fame and the map.

## Primera experiencia (First Experience)

**Date:** 2022-07-20

**Title:** Primera experiencia (First Experience)

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Language:** Spanish

**Themes:** Youth, Awakening, First

## Synopsis: Primera experiencia (First Experience) — Stefan Zweig

Primera experiencia says first experience, in Spanish. Beginnings of love, work, or knowledge are all compatible, and no author lets us pick.

The word first is the pathos. Something happens that cannot happen the same way again.

It is a book of initiation. The domain of the initiation is inside.

## Tres poetas, de su vida (Three Poets, of Their Life)

**Date:** 2022-07-20

**Title:** Tres poetas, de su vida (Three Poets, of Their Life)

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Language:** Spanish

**Themes:** Lives, Writers, Experience

## Synopsis: Tres poetas, de su vida (Three Poets, of Their Life) — Stefan Zweig

Tres poetas, de su vida is Zweig's three poets of their lives: Casanova, Stendhal, and Tolstoy. He is interested in men who tried to make living itself a conscious art, and in the different prices.

Seduction, ambition, and a late conversion are three answers. The essays are swift portraits.

The Spanish and English titles name the same project.
