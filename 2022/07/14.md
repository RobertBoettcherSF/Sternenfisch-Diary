# 2022-07-14 — Brazil and Hatha Yoga

**Date:** 2022-07-14

**Title:** Brazil

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Exile, Future, Country

## Synopsis: Brazil — Stefan Zweig

Brazil is Stefan Zweig's essay on Brazil, written after he had left Europe. He presents the country as a possible future, a place not yet organized around the hatreds he was fleeing.

The portrait is hopeful and incomplete, the view of a newcomer who wants to believe in what he has found.

It belongs with the last part of his life, beside the memoir of the world he had lost.

**Date:** 2022-07-14

**Title:** Hatha Yoga

**Author:** Max Wilke

**Themes:** Yoga, Body, Practice

## Synopsis: Hatha Yoga — Max Wilke

Hatha Yoga is Max Wilke's book on Hatha yoga, the physical discipline of postures and breath. The title says what the instruction is. A specific sequence or claim of results will not be invented here.

A reader looking for practice rather than for a story is the person this title addresses. The body is the subject.

Treat it as a handbook in a tradition, and let the pages supply the method.
