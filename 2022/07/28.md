## Synopsis: The Delusion and the Dreams in W. Jensen's Gradiva — Sigmund Freud

The Delusion and the Dreams in W. Jensen's Gradiva is Wilhelm Jensen's novella of an archaeologist possessed by a relief of a walking woman, and by a dream that sends him to Pompeii to find her. Sigmund Freud's name on the edition marks the famous reading of that obsession.

The story itself is a romance of scholarship and delusion: marble, ashes, and a living girl who does not fit the theory.

It is read now both as a tale and as the narrative Freud used to think about wish and memory.
