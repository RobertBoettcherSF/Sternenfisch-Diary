## Amok

**Date:** 2022-07-04

**Title:** Amok

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Passion, Shame, Exile

## Synopsis: Amok — Stefan Zweig

Amok is Stefan Zweig's novella of a doctor in the tropics who breaks every rule he lives by for a woman who will not be managed. He tells the story afterward, on a ship, as a confession he can barely finish.

Heat, secrecy, and professional ruin press the plot into a few days. The title's word, amok, is the loss of measure, not a romance.

What remains is the cost of a passion that starts as help and ends as pursuit.

## De triomf en tragedie van Erasmus van Rotterdam

**Date:** 2022-07-04

**Title:** De triomf en tragedie van Erasmus van Rotterdam

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Tolerance, Scholarship, Conflict

## Synopsis: De triomf en tragedie van Erasmus van Rotterdam — Stefan Zweig

De triomf en tragedie van Erasmus van Rotterdam is Zweig's Erasmus, the scholar who wanted Christendom repaired without a war of saints. The edition's language changes. The quarrel with fanaticism does not.

Erasmus writes, hesitates, and is overrun by people who have chosen certainty. Zweig clearly prefers the hesitation.

The book is a defense of the intellectual who will not pick up the correct weapon.

## Joseph Fouché

**Date:** 2022-07-04

**Title:** Joseph Fouché

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Power, Betrayal, Politics

## Synopsis: Joseph Fouché — Stefan Zweig

Joseph Fouché is Stefan Zweig's portrait of Joseph Fouché, the politician who survived every change of regime in revolutionary France. He moves from radical to minister of police, serving and outlasting the men who thought they owned him.

Zweig is less interested in a list of offices than in the temperament of a survivor: cold, useful, and never last to leave a sinking alliance. Napoleon is the great antagonist, not the hero.

The book reads as a study of power without loyalty, and of how a secondary figure can steer an age by refusing to be identified with it.

## Little man

**Date:** 2022-07-04

**Title:** Little man

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Marriage, Unemployment, City

## Synopsis: Little man — Hans Fallada

Little man is Hans Fallada's novel of a young couple trying to stay decent in a city that will not employ them. A pregnancy, a thin wage, and the relatives who judge them turn a love story into an economic one.

Fallada's gift is the price of a sausage and the tone of a landlord. Politics sits in the kitchen.

The title's question, what now, is practical. The book refuses a glamorous answer.

## Los grandes momentos de la humanidad

**Date:** 2022-07-04

**Title:** Los grandes momentos de la humanidad

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** History, Crisis, Miniature

## Synopsis: Los grandes momentos de la humanidad — Stefan Zweig

Los grandes momentos de la humanidad is Zweig's great moments of humankind, brief historical narratives, here in Spanish or English as the edition has it. He isolates an hour in which a larger fate tilts.

The method is dramatic compression. A reader does not get the whole war. The reader gets the hinge.

They are essays that behave like stories.
