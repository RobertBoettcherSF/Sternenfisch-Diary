## Synopsis: The Wonders of Life — Stefan Zweig

The Wonders of Life is Stefan Zweig's essayistic look at the wonders of life, curiosity turned toward the natural and the human rather than toward a single biography. Wonder, for him, is an educated emotion.

The pages belong with his cultural prose. They are not a novel and not a textbook.

A reader gets his characteristic warmth in the presence of anything intensely alive.
