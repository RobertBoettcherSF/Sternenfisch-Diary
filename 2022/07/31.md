# 2022-07-31 — La Colección Invisible, La madre del mundo indio, Shasekishū, Sobre los escritores, and The Indian World Mother

**Date:** 2022-07-31

**Title:** La Colección Invisible

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Collection, Pride, Ruin

## Synopsis: La Colección Invisible — Stefan Zweig

La Colección Invisible is Zweig's invisible collection: an old man displays treasures that have already been sold, and everyone who loves him agrees to see them. Inflation is the economic fact. Tenderness and self-deception are the human ones.

The story is very short. It does not need to be longer.

Art, in this tale, is what a person believes is still on the wall.

**Date:** 2022-07-31

**Title:** La madre del mundo indio

**Author:** Heinrich Zimmer

**Themes:** Myth, Mother, India

## Synopsis: La madre del mundo indio — Heinrich Zimmer

La madre del mundo indio names the Indian world-mother, a figure Zimmer follows through myth as power, birth, and terror, not as a gentle abstraction. The feminine divine in these traditions creates and devours.

The Spanish edition under the twin title is the same subject. Comparative myth is the method.

Readers do not need a creed to follow the argument. They need patience for symbols that refuse to be only symbols.

**Date:** 2022-07-31

**Title:** Shasekishū

**Author:** Nyogen Senzaki

**Themes:** Zen, Anecdote, Teaching

## Synopsis: Shasekishū — Nyogen Senzaki

Shasekishū is Shasekishū, Sand and Pebbles, the medieval Japanese collection of Buddhist anecdotes, here under Nyogen Senzaki's name. The pieces are short teachings: a monk, a mistake, a joke that turns into doctrine.

Senzaki was a Zen teacher who presented such material for modern listeners. The edition is a bridge, not a novel.

The right way to read it is a few pebbles at a time. Each anecdote is small and meant to lodge.

**Date:** 2022-07-31

**Title:** Sobre los escritores

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Writers, Essay, Reading

## Synopsis: Sobre los escritores — Stefan Zweig

Sobre los escritores is Stefan Zweig on writers, critical and biographical prose rather than his letters to them. He approaches an author as a fate that found a style.

The pieces are encounters, not a system of literary theory. Warmth and drama lead.

Readers who like his portraits of Balzac or Dickens will recognize the method, aimed here at a shelf of other lives.
