## Synopsis: La cabaña del tío Tom — Harriett Beecher Stowe

La cabaña del tío Tom is Stowe's Uncle Tom's Cabin in Spanish. The novel was written to make the sale of human beings feel like a household event, not a distant policy.

Flight, faith, and cruelty are distributed across several plots so that a reader cannot keep only one comfortable example.

It is openly a book with a purpose, and the purpose is not hidden by the story.
