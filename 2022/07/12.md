# 2022-07-12 — Brazil, Manifesto, Notas sobre enfermería, Revolution, and Wage

**Date:** 2022-07-12

**Title:** Brazil

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Exile, Future, Country

## Synopsis: Brazil — Stefan Zweig

Brazil is Stefan Zweig's essay on Brazil, written after he had left Europe. He presents the country as a possible future, a place not yet organized around the hatreds he was fleeing.

The portrait is hopeful and incomplete, the view of a newcomer who wants to believe in what he has found.

It belongs with the last part of his life, beside the memoir of the world he had lost.

**Date:** 2022-07-12

**Title:** Manifesto

**Author:** Unknown

**Themes:** Declaration, Aims, Public

## Synopsis: Manifesto — Unknown

Manifesto is a manifesto: a public declaration of what should change and why the present will not do. The form is argument, an invitation to agree and to act.

Art, politics, and belief have all used this form. The heat is the point. A manifesto wants company, not a private ending.

A reader should expect claims, a tone of urgency, and a picture of a better arrangement.

**Date:** 2022-07-12

**Title:** Notas sobre enfermería

**Author:** Florence Nigthingale

**Themes:** Care, Sickroom, Attention

## Synopsis: Notas sobre enfermería — Florence Nigthingale

Notas sobre enfermería is Nightingale's notes on nursing in translation. Clean air, quiet, light, and honest observation are treated as lifesaving, not as niceties.

She is sharp about the harm done by well-meaning noise and neglect. The patient is the center.

The book remains a standard for the ethics of staying in the room.

**Date:** 2022-07-12

**Title:** Revolution

**Author:** Karl Marx

**Themes:** Revolution, Class, Politics

## Synopsis: Revolution — Karl Marx

Revolution names revolution and names Karl Marx. Which text sits under so broad a title is not something to guess from the word alone.

What can be said without invention is the field: political upheaval, class power, and the replacement of an old order, the questions Marx spent his public life on.

The pages belong with political argument, not with a story that has characters and a private ending.

**Date:** 2022-07-12

**Title:** Wage

**Author:** Karl Marx

**Themes:** Labor, Wages, Capital

## Synopsis: Wage — Karl Marx

Wage carries Karl Marx's name and the single subject of the wage. His writing on that subject treats pay not as a fair day's simple exchange but as the price of labor-power inside a larger relation.

This edition's short title does not by itself identify which lecture or pamphlet is inside. The note therefore stays with the theme the title and the author jointly announce.

A reader should expect an argument about work and money, not a novel and not a neutral handbook.
