**Date:** 2022-07-19

**Title:** Amerigo

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Exploration, Name, America

## Synopsis: Amerigo — Stefan Zweig

Amerigo is Stefan Zweig's portrait of Amerigo Vespucci, the man whose first name was given to two continents. Zweig is interested in how a navigator's letters became a baptism for a world.

The drama is reputation as much as seamanship. A name sticks, and the sticking is a historical event of its own.

It is a short biographical essay in Zweig's swift manner, about fame and the map.

**Date:** 2022-07-19

**Title:** First experience

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Youth, Awakening, Stories

## Synopsis: First experience — Stefan Zweig

First experience is Stefan Zweig's First Experience, stories of erotic and emotional awakening. One edition's subtitle names four stories from the land of childhood. The theme is the first time a feeling becomes destiny.

He writes these initiations without smirking. Shame and wonder are both allowed.

They are early work, already aimed at the psychological novella he later mastered.
