## Alhambra

**Date:** 2022-07-08

**Title:** Alhambra

**Author:** Washington Irving

**Themes:** Spain, Legend, Travel

## Synopsis: Alhambra — Washington Irving

Alhambra is Washington Irving's book of residence in the Alhambra, mixing travel sketch, history, and legends told in the palace. He treats the courts and the people who still live among them with a visitor's delight and a historian's appetite.

Tales of hidden treasure, enchanted soldiers, and old Moorish kings sit beside notes on the life of Granada.

It is a romantic book in the best sense: a real place allowed to keep its stories.

## Gårdagens värld (The World of Yesterday)

**Date:** 2022-07-08

**Title:** Gårdagens värld (The World of Yesterday)

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Language:** Swedish

**Themes:** Memory, Yesterday, Loss

## Synopsis: Gårdagens värld (The World of Yesterday) — Stefan Zweig

Gårdagens värld says the world of yesterday. In several languages that phrase is a book's name for a civilization remembered after it had broken: Vienna, the habits of a peaceful Europe, the shock of what followed.

Where this edition names Stefan Zweig, the memoir is his. Where the author line is empty, the title still points at that kind of remembrance, a life used as a witness to a vanished common world.

It is not a battle history. It is a book of rooms, friendships, and borders that used to be easy.

## Las aventuras de Alicia (Alice's Adventures in Wonderland)

**Date:** 2022-07-08

**Title:** Las aventuras de Alicia (Alice's Adventures in Wonderland)

**Author:** Lewis Carroll

**Language:** Spanish

**Themes:** Nonsense, Child, Logic

## Synopsis: Las aventuras de Alicia (Alice's Adventures in Wonderland) — Lewis Carroll

Las aventuras de Alicia is Carroll's Alice in the language of this edition. A child falls among rules that are internally consistent and externally mad, and she tries to be both polite and precise.

The jokes are linguistic. They survive translation to the extent that the translator is willing to play.

It is still a book to read aloud.

## Novela de ajedrez (Chess Novella)

**Date:** 2022-07-08

**Title:** Novela de ajedrez (Chess Novella)

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Language:** Spanish

**Themes:** Chess, Isolation, Mind

## Synopsis: Novela de ajedrez (Chess Novella) — Stefan Zweig

Novela de ajedrez is Zweig's chess novella in translation. A brutal champion and a man who learned the game in solitary confinement sit down on a ship. The game is the least of what is being played.

Confinement has trained a mind in a way that is both a salvation and a damage. The match brings the damage back.

It is one of the last things Zweig wrote, and it feels like it.

## The soldier trade of German princes to America

**Date:** 2022-07-08

**Title:** The soldier trade of German princes to America

**Author:** Friedrich Kapp

**Themes:** War, Trade, America

## Synopsis: The soldier trade of German princes to America — Friedrich Kapp

The soldier trade of German princes to America is Friedrich Kapp's history of the trade by which German princes rented their soldiers to fight in the American Revolutionary War. Men were a line in a treasury book. The book looks at that fact without romance.

Kapp writes as a historian of German-American life, angry at the petty sovereignties that sold a regiment. The politics of the subsidy system matter as much as the battles.

It is a documentary narrative about empire, cash, and the people shipped across an ocean under someone else's flag.
