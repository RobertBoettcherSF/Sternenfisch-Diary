**Date:** 2022-07-22

**Title:** Cartas a los escritores

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Letters, Literature, Friendship

## Synopsis: Cartas a los escritores — Stefan Zweig

Cartas a los escritores gathers Stefan Zweig's correspondence with other writers. The interest is the working life of letters: encouragement, disagreement, exile, and the practical business of books.

These are not a single narrative. They show a man who lived through other people's manuscripts as well as his own.

What a browser finds is the tone of a literary friendship, attentive and often urgent.

**Date:** 2022-07-22

**Title:** Castellio contra Calvin

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Conscience, Heresy, Tolerance

## Synopsis: Castellio contra Calvin — Stefan Zweig

Castellio contra Calvin is Zweig's account of Castellio against Calvin, in translation. A man is burned for his theology, and another man says that the burning is the real heresy.

The book takes sides. Tolerance is not portrayed as a mild preference but as a fought position.

It is history written because the author thought the argument was not over.

**Date:** 2022-07-22

**Title:** El amor de Erika Ewald

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Music, Youth, Love

## Synopsis: El amor de Erika Ewald — Stefan Zweig

El amor de Erika Ewald is Zweig's love of Erika Ewald, an early novella of a young woman in the world of music, here in Spanish. A feeling arrives before the character has a life large enough to hold it.

The scale is chamber music, not orchestra. That is a description of the emotional size as well.

It points toward the later, harsher novellas without yet being one of them.

**Date:** 2022-07-22

**Title:** La curación a través del espíritu

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Healing, Belief, Mind

## Synopsis: La curación a través del espíritu — Stefan Zweig

La curación a través del espíritu is Zweig's healing through the spirit, in translation: Mesmer, Mary Baker Eddy, and Freud as three claims that a mind can cure. He is curious and not devout.

The subject is authority over suffering. Each figure builds a method and a following.

It is essayistic biography, grouped by a single question.

**Date:** 2022-07-22

**Title:** Textos breves sobre personalidades históricas

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** History, Portrait, Essay

## Synopsis: Textos breves sobre personalidades históricas — Stefan Zweig

Textos breves sobre personalidades históricas collects Zweig's short texts on historical personalities, brief lives rather than archives. He looks for the gesture or the crisis that explains a public figure.

The method is the same as in his longer biographies, compressed. Sympathy and drama lead; the footnote does not.

It is a book of portraits for readers who like history as character.
