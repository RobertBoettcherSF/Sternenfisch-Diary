# 2022-05-12 — De triomf en tragedie van Erasmus van Rotterdam, Trip to Russia, and Uli the tenant

**Date:** 2022-05-12

**Title:** De triomf en tragedie van Erasmus van Rotterdam

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Tolerance, Scholarship, Conflict

## Synopsis: De triomf en tragedie van Erasmus van Rotterdam — Stefan Zweig

De triomf en tragedie van Erasmus van Rotterdam is Zweig's Erasmus, the scholar who wanted Christendom repaired without a war of saints. The edition's language changes. The quarrel with fanaticism does not.

Erasmus writes, hesitates, and is overrun by people who have chosen certainty. Zweig clearly prefers the hesitation.

The book is a defense of the intellectual who will not pick up the correct weapon.

**Date:** 2022-05-12

**Title:** Trip to Russia

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Russia, Travel, Writers

## Synopsis: Trip to Russia — Stefan Zweig

Trip to Russia is Zweig's account of a trip to Russia, curious, literary, and personal. He goes as a European man of letters meeting a world he has mostly known through its novels.

The value is the traveler's eye, not a political system explained. Encounters and atmospheres do the work.

It belongs with his other travel pages, before exile made travel a different kind of fact.

**Date:** 2022-05-12

**Title:** Uli the tenant

**Author:** Jeremias Gotthelf

**Themes:** Farm, Responsibility, Marriage

## Synopsis: Uli the tenant — Jeremias Gotthelf

Uli the tenant is Gotthelf's later Uli book, when the servant has become a tenant and the harder test begins: land, debt, a household, and the character required to hold them. Getting the farm is not the same as deserving it.

Gotthelf remains practical and moral at once. A man's treatment of work, wife, and money is the plot.

It continues the story of a rise in the world, and it is less interested in the rise than in whether the man stays sound.
