## Synopsis: Martin Salander — Gottfried Keller

Martin Salander is Gottfried Keller's novel of Martin Salander, a Swiss democrat who comes home from abroad full of faith in public life and is steadily instructed by fraud, fashion, and his own household. The optimism is real. So is the fleecing.

Keller watches a liberal century congratulate itself. A son's slickness and a community's speculation do more damage than any villain from outside.

It is a political novel in the dress of a family story, written by a man who had served the same kind of republic he now scolds.
