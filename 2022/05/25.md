# 2022-05-25 — Fragments, Pride and Prejudice, Romain Rolland, Stolz und Vorurteil, and The Peasant Mirror

## Fragments

**Date:** 2022-05-25

**Title:** Fragments

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Fragments, Notebooks, Aphorism

## Synopsis: Fragments — Franz Kafka

Fragments draws on Kafka's notebooks and unfinished pages. These are not polished tales so much as openings, refusals, and sentences that stop when the thought has shown its edge.

The interest is the workshop: how a legal or family dread finds a first image.

They reward slow reading, a page or two at a time.

## Pride and Prejudice

**Date:** 2022-05-25

**Title:** Pride and Prejudice

**Author:** Jane Austen

**Themes:** Marriage, Class, Pride

## Synopsis: Pride and Prejudice — Jane Austen

Pride and Prejudice follows the Bennet sisters, whose futures depend on whom they marry and what money comes with the match. Elizabeth Bennet is quick and stubborn. Mr. Darcy is wealthy, awkward in company, and easy to dislike on a first evening.

Balls, visits, a bad proposal, and a letter that rewrites Elizabeth's reading of him carry the story. Around them sit a foolish clergyman, a runaway sister, and an estate that looks like security until character is tested.

The pleasure is the slow correction of a first impression, and a marriage that becomes possible only after both people have been wrong in public.

## Romain Rolland

**Date:** 2022-05-25

**Title:** Romain Rolland

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Biography, Pacifism, Music

## Synopsis: Romain Rolland — Stefan Zweig

Romain Rolland is Stefan Zweig's portrait of Romain Rolland, the French writer and music scholar he admired as a European conscience. The life and the stance against nationalist hatred are the subject.

Some editions add that this is the man and the work. That double attention is Zweig's usual method: the books inside the biography.

It is an essay of friendship as much as of criticism.

## Stolz und Vorurteil (Pride and Prejudice)

**Date:** 2022-05-25

**Title:** Stolz und Vorurteil (Pride and Prejudice)

**Author:** Jane Austen

**Language:** German

**Themes:** Marriage, Pride, Manners

## Synopsis: Stolz und Vorurteil (Pride and Prejudice) — Jane Austen

Stolz und Vorurteil is Jane Austen's novel of Elizabeth Bennet and Mr. Darcy, in the language of this edition. A family of daughters must marry, a proud man must be reread, and a witty woman must discover that her first accuracy was incomplete.

Money, embarrassment, and a great house do the structural work. The comedy is the correction of a confident mistake.

The story does not depend on the English title to be itself. The characters survive the crossing.

## The Peasant Mirror

**Date:** 2022-05-25

**Title:** The Peasant Mirror

**Author:** Jeremias Gotthelf

**Themes:** Autobiography, Farmer, Vocation

## Synopsis: The Peasant Mirror — Jeremias Gotthelf

The Peasant Mirror presents itself as a life of Jeremias Gotthelf told by himself, a mirror held up to a peasant world and to the pastor-novelist who described it. Memory and example are the method.

The subtitle on some editions makes the self-portrait explicit. A reader gets the man and the parishes, not a disguised romance.

It is the right book if you want the author's own frame around the village novels.
