**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Law, Guilt, Bureaucracy

## Synopsis: The Trial — Franz Kafka

The Trial opens with an arrest that names no charge. Josef K. spends the rest of the book trying to find the court that has claimed him, in attics, stairwells, and conversations that explain nothing usable.

Every attempt to be reasonable feeds the procedure. Helpers, women, a painter, and a priest offer interpretations that do not free him.

The novel is about a guilt that arrives before the reason, and about offices that exist to prolong the case.
