**Title:** Afterwards

**Author:** Kurt Tucholsky

**Themes:** Aftermath, Memory, Tone

## Synopsis: Afterwards — Kurt Tucholsky

Afterwards is one of Tucholsky's shorter books of reflection after the event, the mood of afterward rather than of the party itself. Exact contents vary by edition; the title's promise is the residue.

He was a master of the note that sounds casual and leaves a bruise. Expect that, not a treatise.

It belongs with the late, more shadowed side of his work.
