**Date:** 2022-05-04

**Title:** Mary Stuart

**Author:** Friedrich Schiller

**Themes:** Queen, History, Captivity

## Synopsis: Mary Stuart — Friedrich Schiller

Mary Stuart names Mary Stuart. Plays, biographies, and partisan histories have all used that name, and this edition does not say which kind it is or who wrote it.

The historical person is not in doubt: a Scottish queen, a claim, a long English captivity, a death that became a symbol. The book will choose its emphasis.

A browser knows the figure. The interpretation is the reason to read this one rather than another.

**Date:** 2022-05-04

**Title:** The Field Gunner

**Author:** Unknown

**Themes:** War, Soldier, Craft

## Synopsis: The Field Gunner — Unknown

The Field Gunner is a field gunner, an artilleryman whose work is a trade as much as a battle. No author is given, so no campaign will be narrated.

The interest signaled is military and concrete: guns, ground, the person who serves the piece. Glory is not required by the wording.

It reads as a soldier's book, or a book about a soldier's job.
