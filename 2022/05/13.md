## Synopsis: Novellas and stories — Stefan Zweig

Novellas and stories is a collection of Zweig's novellas and stories, the genre he used for lives that tip. The pieces are independent. What they share is a crisis told at close range.

Travel, music, shame, and sudden confession recur. He likes a frame: someone telling the tale after the damage.

A new reader can enter anywhere. Each story is built to be finished in an evening.

**Title:** The healing through the spirit

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Healing, Faith, Biography

## Synopsis: The healing through the spirit — Stefan Zweig

The healing through the spirit looks at three figures who claimed a power to heal through spirit rather than through ordinary medicine: Mesmer, Mary Baker Eddy, and Freud. Zweig sets magnetism, Christian Science, and psychoanalysis side by side.

He is interested in the authority a suffering person grants to a healer, and in the theories built to explain that authority.

The essays are skeptical and absorbed at once. They ask what cure means when the instrument is belief.
