# 2022-05-24 — Wolf among wolves and Zeitgeist and Bernese Spirit

## Wolf among wolves

**Date:** 2022-05-24

**Title:** Wolf among wolves

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Inflation, Survival, Berlin

## Synopsis: Wolf among wolves — Hans Fallada

Wolf among wolves is Fallada's long novel of the German inflation, when money died by the hour and character was priced accordingly. Farmers, officers, addicts, and schemers cross in a plot of survival and revenge.

The city and the country are both unsafe. Love and loyalty are tested against the simple need to eat next week.

The two parts, city and countryside, are one argument about a society that has lost its measure.

## Zeitgeist and Bernese Spirit

**Date:** 2022-05-24

**Title:** Zeitgeist and Bernese Spirit

**Author:** Jeremias Gotthelf

**Themes:** Tradition, Faith, Change

## Synopsis: Zeitgeist and Bernese Spirit — Jeremias Gotthelf

Zeitgeist and Bernese Spirit sets the fashionable spirit of the age against a Bernese spirit Gotthelf thought more solid. He was a pastor who distrusted easy progress and wrote novels to argue.

The conflict is cultural: new ideas arriving in a countryside with its own hard-won habits. He is not neutral.

Reading him here means reading a conservative imagination at full strength, specific and alive.
