# 2022-08-11 — Así habló Zaratustra, Ecce homo, El Anticristo, Nochebuena, Pointers - Intentions, Rheinsberg, The Antichrist, and Thus Spoke Zarathustra

**Date:** 2022-08-11

**Title:** Así habló Zaratustra

**Author:** Friedrich Nietzsche

**Themes:** Teaching, Value, Voice

## Synopsis: Así habló Zaratustra — Friedrich Nietzsche

Así habló Zaratustra is Nietzsche's Thus Spoke Zarathustra in translation. A teacher comes down from solitude with parables about creating values after the old ones have failed.

The form is closer to scripture and dithyramb than to an essay. That is a difficulty and the book's chosen power.

It is Nietzsche at his most prophetic, and he knows the danger of the pose.

**Title:** Ecce homo

**Author:** Friedrich Nietzsche

**Themes:** Autobiography, Philosophy, Style

## Synopsis: Ecce homo — Friedrich Nietzsche

Ecce homo is Nietzsche writing about his own books, in a voice that boasts, explains, and dares the reader to misunderstand him. Each major work gets a chapter that is part guide and part performance.

The famous provocations sit beside practical remarks on climate, diet, and how he wants to be read.

It is the strangest of autobiographies: a philosopher reviewing his life as if it were a set of titles.

**Date:** 2022-08-11

**Title:** El Anticristo

**Author:** Friedrich Nietzsche

**Themes:** Religion, Critique, Christianity

## Synopsis: El Anticristo — Friedrich Nietzsche

El Anticristo is Nietzsche's late, violent polemic against Christianity as he understood it: a religion of weakness enthroned as goodness. He writes to injure a piety, not to balance a history of the church.

The tone is pamphlet, not system. Pity, the priest, and the reversal of ancient values are the targets.

It is short, intemperate, and meant to be.

**Date:** 2022-08-11

**Title:** Nochebuena

**Themes:** Christmas, Night, Feast

## Synopsis: Nochebuena

Nochebuena is Nochebuena, the night of Christmas Eve. Feast, family, a journey home, or a wonder that happens because the night is set apart: the title allows all of these and specifies none.

No author is named. No particular miracle will be claimed. The calendar is the atmosphere.

It is a seasonal book, or a story that needed that night in order to happen.

**Title:** Pointers - Intentions

**Author:** Oscar Wilde

**Themes:** Criticism, Art, Dialogue

## Synopsis: Pointers - Intentions — Oscar Wilde

Pointers - Intentions collects Oscar Wilde's critical dialogues and essays, above all his defense of lying in art and of the critic as a maker rather than a servant of the work. Conversation is the form.

He argues that life imitates art more than the other way around, and he enjoys the paradox enough to make it a method.

The book is theory written as performance, which is the only kind of theory he trusted.

**Date:** 2022-08-11

**Title:** Rheinsberg

**Author:** Kurt Tucholsky

**Themes:** Love, Weekend, Youth

## Synopsis: Rheinsberg — Kurt Tucholsky

Rheinsberg is Kurt Tucholsky's light story of a young couple who steal a weekend in Rheinsberg and play at being more grown than they are. The book is small on purpose.

Flirtation, a hotel, and the pleasure of a borrowed sophistication make the comedy. Under it is real affection.

It has stayed popular because it is happy without being stupid about happiness.

**Title:** Thus Spoke Zarathustra

**Author:** Friedrich Nietzsche

**Themes:** Prophet, Value, Self-overcoming

## Synopsis: Thus Spoke Zarathustra — Friedrich Nietzsche

Thus Spoke Zarathustra is Nietzsche's philosophical drama in which Zarathustra comes down from a mountain to talk about the death of old values and the difficult invention of new ones. The form is parable, song, and speech, not a treatise.

The Übermensch, the eternal recurrence, and the attack on comfortable virtue are staged as teachings a crowd mostly cannot use.

It is the book where Nietzsche risks a scripture of his own, and argues with it as he writes.
