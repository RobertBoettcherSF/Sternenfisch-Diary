# 2022-08-25 — Back then at home, Entonces en casa, Hoy en casa - Otro libro -, and Today at home

## Back then at home

**Date:** 2022-08-25

**Title:** Back then at home

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Childhood, Memory, Home

## Synopsis: Back then at home — Hans Fallada

Back then at home belongs to Hans Fallada's books of home, sketches of the household he came from and the one he later made. Anecdote, not thesis, is the unit.

The charm is specific: rooms, relatives, the small operations of a German domestic life across troubled decades. He does not pretend coziness is the whole truth.

These pages are memoir in pieces, approachable, and best read as reminiscence rather than as a novel with a single crisis.

## Entonces en casa (Back Then at Home)

**Date:** 2022-08-25

**Title:** Entonces en casa (Back Then at Home)

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Language:** Spanish

**Themes:** Home, Memory, Past

## Synopsis: Entonces en casa (Back Then at Home) — Hans Fallada

Entonces en casa says 'back then at home.' Memory of a household is the subject the words announce. No author is named, so no particular family will be moved in from another book.

The tense is retrospective. Something about that home is over, or at least far enough away to be told.

It is a book of remembrance. The rooms belong to the text.

## Hoy en casa - Otro libro - (Today at Home: Another Book)

**Date:** 2022-08-25

**Title:** Hoy en casa - Otro libro - (Today at Home: Another Book)

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Language:** Spanish

**Themes:** Home, Daily, Book

## Synopsis: Hoy en casa - Otro libro - (Today at Home: Another Book) — Hans Fallada

Hoy en casa - Otro libro - says 'at home today,' and the subtitle calls it another book. Domestic life in the present, or in a present being written down, is the offer. No author is attached in the record.

A sequel-feeling is in the phrase 'another book,' a return to a house already described. The note will not invent the earlier volume's events.

Expect the scale of a household, not of a nation, unless the pages surprise you.
