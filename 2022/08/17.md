# 2022-08-17 — Así habló Zaratustra and Nietzsche versus Wagner

**Date:** 2022-08-17

**Title:** Así habló Zaratustra

**Author:** Friedrich Nietzsche

**Themes:** Teaching, Value, Voice

## Synopsis: Así habló Zaratustra — Friedrich Nietzsche

Así habló Zaratustra is Nietzsche's Thus Spoke Zarathustra in translation. A teacher comes down from solitude with parables about creating values after the old ones have failed.

The form is closer to scripture and dithyramb than to an essay. That is a difficulty and the book's chosen power.

It is Nietzsche at his most prophetic, and he knows the danger of the pose.

**Date:** 2022-08-17

**Title:** Nietzsche versus Wagner

**Author:** Friedrich Nietzsche

**Themes:** Music, Wagner, Critique

## Synopsis: Nietzsche versus Wagner — Friedrich Nietzsche

Nietzsche versus Wagner collects Nietzsche's break with Richard Wagner. Early devotion turns into a diagnosis: the composer as a sickness, a theater of redemption that Nietzsche no longer trusts.

The pieces are personal. A friendship and a theory of art come apart in the same pages.

Readers do not need to take his side to see how seriously he had once listened.
