# 2022-08-28 — He who once eats from a tin bowl and Iron Gustav

**Date:** 2022-08-28

**Title:** He who once eats from a tin bowl

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Prison, Return, Stigma

## Synopsis: He who once eats from a tin bowl — Hans Fallada

He who once eats from a tin bowl is Fallada's novel of a man who has been in prison and finds that release is another kind of sentence. Work, lodging, and respectability are organized to remind him of the tin bowl.

The book is angry about a society that preaches reform and then blocks every door. Small humiliations do the structural work.

It is a story about whether a person is allowed to be done with his punishment.

**Date:** 2022-08-28

**Title:** Iron Gustav

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Berlin, War, Family

## Synopsis: Iron Gustav — Hans Fallada

Iron Gustav follows Gustav, a Berlin cabby who believes in work, the Kaiser, and the straight line of his own life, through the war that breaks those beliefs. Fallada uses one family to cross from empire to inflation and street fighting.

Sons take different roads, including one into the new politics of resentment. The father cannot read the map.

It is a big, concrete novel of a city changing faster than a honest trade.
