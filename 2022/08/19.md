**Title:** Epistola in Carcere et Vinculis

**Author:** Oscar Wilde

**Themes:** Prison, Love, Art

## Synopsis: Epistola in Carcere et Vinculis — Oscar Wilde

Epistola in Carcere et Vinculis is Wilde's De Profundis, the prison letter, under the Latin title or the explicit 'letter from prison' title. It is a reckoning with the love and the self-display that led to his conviction.

Christ, art, and humiliation are thought about at length. The style does not become plain just because the room is.

It is one of the great documents of a ruined celebrity trying to become a soul.

**Date:** 2022-08-19

**Title:** Fairy Tales

**Author:** Oscar Wilde

**Themes:** Fairy Tale, Moral, Wit

## Synopsis: Fairy Tales — Oscar Wilde

Fairy Tales gathers Oscar Wilde's fairy tales, stories in which a splendid surface is asked to pay for someone else's suffering. Princes, giants, rockets, and statues learn or fail in a few pages.

The wit does not cancel the ache. He writes for children in sentences adults recognize as moral criticism.

They are best read one at a time. Each is a small, finished object.

**Date:** 2022-08-19

**Title:** Tales and aphorisms

**Themes:** Tale, Aphorism, Brevity

## Synopsis: Tales and aphorisms

Tales and aphorisms pairs tales and aphorisms, two short forms, and names no author. Stories with a point, and sentences that are the point, share a cover.

No sample wisdom will be coined here to fill the gap. Brevity is the contract.

A reader who likes to stop after a page and keep a line is the person this title is for.
