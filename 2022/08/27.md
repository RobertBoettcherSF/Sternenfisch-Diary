# 2022-08-27 — He who once eats from a tin bowl and Peasants, bigwigs & bombs

**Date:** 2022-08-27

**Title:** He who once eats from a tin bowl

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Prison, Return, Stigma

## Synopsis: He who once eats from a tin bowl — Hans Fallada

He who once eats from a tin bowl is Fallada's novel of a man who has been in prison and finds that release is another kind of sentence. Work, lodging, and respectability are organized to remind him of the tin bowl.

The book is angry about a society that preaches reform and then blocks every door. Small humiliations do the structural work.

It is a story about whether a person is allowed to be done with his punishment.

**Date:** 2022-08-27

**Title:** Peasants, bigwigs & bombs

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Village, Politics, Protest

## Synopsis: Peasants, bigwigs & bombs — Hans Fallada

Peasants, bigwigs & bombs is Fallada's novel of a provincial town where farmers, party bosses, and newspapers turn a local grievance into bombs and bargains. He knew this world as a reporter and a defendant.

Nobody is allowed a clean motive for long. The comedy of small-town vanity curdles into political danger.

It is an early, tough book about how public life actually feels in a district, not in a capital.
