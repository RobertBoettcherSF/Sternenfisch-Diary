# 2022-08-12 — Beyond Good and Evil and Ecce homo

## Beyond Good and Evil

**Date:** 2022-08-12

**Title:** Beyond Good and Evil

**Author:** Friedrich Nietzsche

**Themes:** Morality, Philosophy, Power

## Synopsis: Beyond Good and Evil — Friedrich Nietzsche

Beyond Good and Evil is Nietzsche's attack on the moral habits he thought Europe had inherited without examining. He asks who benefits from a virtue, and whether 'good' has been defined by the injured or by the strong.

The chapters are aphoristic and combative. Psychology, religion, and scholarship all get the same rude questions.

It is a book for readers willing to watch their own certainties be treated as symptoms.

## Ecce homo

**Date:** 2022-08-12

**Title:** Ecce homo

**Author:** Friedrich Nietzsche

**Themes:** Autobiography, Philosophy, Style

## Synopsis: Ecce homo — Friedrich Nietzsche

Ecce homo is Nietzsche writing about his own books, in a voice that boasts, explains, and dares the reader to misunderstand him. Each major work gets a chapter that is part guide and part performance.

The famous provocations sit beside practical remarks on climate, diet, and how he wants to be read.

It is the strangest of autobiographies: a philosopher reviewing his life as if it were a set of titles.
