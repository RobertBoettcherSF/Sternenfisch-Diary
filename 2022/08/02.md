# 2022-08-02 — Maya and Maya the Indian Myth

## Maya

**Date:** 2022-08-02

**Title:** Maya

**Author:** Heinrich Zimmer

**Themes:** Myth, India, Illusion

## Synopsis: Maya — Heinrich Zimmer

Maya belongs to Heinrich Zimmer's work on Indian myth, and to the idea of maya: the dazzling, deceptive play in which the world appears. He treats myths as living knowledge, not as quaint errors.

Gods, mothers of the world, and cosmic illusion are approached as ways a culture thinks, precise in their own terms. The prose is enthusiastic and learned.

It is a book for readers who want Indian story as philosophy rather than as a list of names.
