## The Fan of Lady Windermere

**Date:** 2022-08-22

**Title:** The Fan of Lady Windermere

**Author:** Oscar Wilde

**Themes:** Society, Reputation, Marriage

## Synopsis: The Fan of Lady Windermere — Oscar Wilde

The Fan of Lady Windermere is the comedy of Lady Windermere's fan: a young wife sure of her moral categories, a husband with a secret, and a woman with a past who refuses to be only a scandal. A fan left in the wrong room almost ruins several lives.

Wilde's epigrams do the glittering work. Under them is a serious dislike of people who enjoy condemning.

The play is a society drama that keeps discovering mercy where the drawing room expected a scene.

## The fight with the demon

**Date:** 2022-08-22

**Title:** The fight with the demon

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Genius, Madness, Poetry

## Synopsis: The fight with the demon — Stefan Zweig

The fight with the demon gathers Zweig's essays on Hölderlin, Kleist, and Nietzsche, three writers he saw as driven past the point where a life can stay intact. The 'demon' of the title is the force that makes the work and breaks the person.

Each essay follows the same curve: early promise, isolation, and a mind that will not settle for a manageable career.

It is criticism written as drama, about the price of an uncompromising inner life.

## Two tender little lambs

**Date:** 2022-08-22

**Title:** Two tender little lambs

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Innocence, Pair, Tenderness

## Synopsis: Two tender little lambs — Hans Fallada

Two tender little lambs names two tender lambs, and with Hans Fallada's byline the expectation is a small-scale story of vulnerable creatures, human or animal, treated without sneer. A fuller plot is not secure enough to retell.

The title's tone is protective. Fallada's usual specificity about ordinary life is the reason to trust the pages rather than a summary.

It is a minor work beside Iron Gustav, and it is allowed to be gentle.
