**Themes:** Berlin, War, Family

## Synopsis: Iron Gustav — Hans Fallada

Iron Gustav follows Gustav, a Berlin cabby who believes in work, the Kaiser, and the straight line of his own life, through the war that breaks those beliefs. Fallada uses one family to cross from empire to inflation and street fighting.

Sons take different roads, including one into the new politics of resentment. The father cannot read the map.

It is a big, concrete novel of a city changing faster than a honest trade.
