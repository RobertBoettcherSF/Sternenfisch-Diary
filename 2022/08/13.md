# 2022-08-13 — Beyond Good and Evil, Más allá del bien y del mal, and Twilight of the Idols

## Beyond Good and Evil

**Date:** 2022-08-13

**Title:** Beyond Good and Evil

**Author:** Friedrich Nietzsche

**Themes:** Morality, Philosophy, Power

## Synopsis: Beyond Good and Evil — Friedrich Nietzsche

Beyond Good and Evil is Nietzsche's attack on the moral habits he thought Europe had inherited without examining. He asks who benefits from a virtue, and whether 'good' has been defined by the injured or by the strong.

The chapters are aphoristic and combative. Psychology, religion, and scholarship all get the same rude questions.

It is a book for readers willing to watch their own certainties be treated as symptoms.

## Más allá del bien y del mal

**Date:** 2022-08-13

**Title:** Más allá del bien y del mal

**Author:** Friedrich Nietzsche

**Themes:** Morality, Rank, Psychology

## Synopsis: Más allá del bien y del mal — Friedrich Nietzsche

Más allá del bien y del mal is Beyond Good and Evil in the language of this edition. Nietzsche treats moral judgments as symptoms and asks what kind of life each virtue protects.

The chapters are sequences of strikes, not a gentle system. A reader is expected to be able to lose an argument with the page.

It is a central book of his mature thought.

## Twilight of the Idols

**Date:** 2022-08-13

**Title:** Twilight of the Idols

**Author:** Friedrich Nietzsche

**Themes:** Idols, Critique, Style

## Synopsis: Twilight of the Idols — Friedrich Nietzsche

Twilight of the Idols is Nietzsche in a hurry, knocking down philosophers, moralists, and German pieties he regarded as idols. The sections are brief, often a page, and written to be remembered as blows.

Socrates, morality as anti-nature, and the confusion of cause with consequence are among the targets.

It is a handbook of his late opinions, faster and meaner than the earlier aphorism books.
