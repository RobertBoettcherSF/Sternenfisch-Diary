**Title:** An Old heart goes on a journey

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Age, Journey, Kindness

## Synopsis: An Old heart goes on a journey — Hans Fallada

An Old heart goes on a journey is Fallada's late story of an old heart that goes traveling. An elderly person sets out, and the journey is a moral experiment in what age still owes the young and itself.

It is warmer than the inflation novels, without becoming soft about need. Fallada still counts the coins.

The appeal is a single, late act of decency given room to matter.

**Date:** 2022-08-24

**Title:** Back then at home

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Childhood, Memory, Home

## Synopsis: Back then at home — Hans Fallada

Back then at home belongs to Hans Fallada's books of home, sketches of the household he came from and the one he later made. Anecdote, not thesis, is the unit.

The charm is specific: rooms, relatives, the small operations of a German domestic life across troubled decades. He does not pretend coziness is the whole truth.

These pages are memoir in pieces, approachable, and best read as reminiscence rather than as a novel with a single crisis.
