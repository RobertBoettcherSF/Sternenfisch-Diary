## Synopsis: Amok: A Novella of Passion — Stefan Zweig

Amok: A Novella of Passion is Stefan Zweig's novella of a doctor in the tropics who breaks every rule he lives by for a woman who will not be managed. He tells the story afterward, on a ship, as a confession he can barely finish.

Heat, secrecy, and professional ruin press the plot into a few days. The title's word, amok, is the loss of measure, not a romance.

What remains is the cost of a passion that starts as help and ends as pursuit.

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Chess, Captivity, Obsession

## Synopsis: Chess novella — Stefan Zweig

Chess novella is Stefan Zweig's novella about a game that becomes a prison. On an ocean liner, a crude world champion meets a stranger whose only training was a chess book memorized in solitary confinement.

The match is short. The real story is how a mind survives when the only opponent left is itself, and what happens when that practice is dragged back into daylight.

Zweig wrote it late, and the cruelty of confinement is the point, not the opening theory.
