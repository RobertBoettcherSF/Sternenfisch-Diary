## Synopsis: Castellio against Calvin: or a conscience against violence — Stefan Zweig

Castellio against Calvin: or a conscience against violence sets Sebastian Castellio against John Calvin after the burning of Michael Servetus. Zweig takes the side of the man who argued that killing a heretic does not defend the truth.

Geneva's discipline, the pamphlets, and the loneliness of the dissenter are the scenery. The book is a polemic in the form of history.

Its question is simple and still uncomfortable: who has the right to punish a belief.
