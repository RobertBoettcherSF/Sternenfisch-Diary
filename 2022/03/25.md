**Title:** Bardo Thodol: El libro tibetano de los muertos

**Author:** W. Y. Evans

**Themes:** Death, Passage, Mind

## Synopsis: Bardo Thodol: El libro tibetano de los muertos — W. Y. Evans

Bardo Thodol: El libro tibetano de los muertos is a language-edition of the Tibetan Book of the Dead, instructions for the interval after dying. Visions come. The teaching says they are the mind's own light, to be recognized rather than feared or worshipped.

The aim is liberation, or, failing that, a less blinded rebirth. The voice is practical about a metaphysical event.

Commentaries differ by translator. The core is a guide for the time when the ordinary self comes apart.

## Synopsis: Brasil: Uma terra do futuro — Stefan Zweig

## Brazil: A country of the future

**Date:** 2022-03-25

**Title:** Brazil: A country of the future

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Exile, Future, Country

## Synopsis: Brazil: A country of the future — Stefan Zweig

Brazil: A country of the future is Stefan Zweig's essay on Brazil, written after he had left Europe. He presents the country as a possible future, a place not yet organized around the hatreds he was fleeing.

The portrait is hopeful and incomplete, the view of a newcomer who wants to believe in what he has found.

It belongs with the last part of his life, beside the memoir of the world he had lost.
