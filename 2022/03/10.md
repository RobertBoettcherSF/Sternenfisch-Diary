## Synopsis: War and peace — Leo Nikolayevich Tolstoy

War and peace moves between the drawing rooms of Moscow and Petersburg and the battlefields of Napoleon's invasion. Tolstoy follows the Rostovs, the Bolkonskys, and Pierre Bezukhov through engagement, ruin, and a search for a life that means something.

The war chapters refuse the idea of a single heroic command. History, in this novel, is a swarm of small decisions.

It is long because the author wants both the ballroom and the theory of how events happen. Readers who stay with it get a whole society.
