**Date:** 2022-09-18

**Title:** Candide

**Author:** Francois-Marie Arouet Voltaire

**Themes:** Satire, Optimism, Travel

## Synopsis: Candide — Francois-Marie Arouet Voltaire

Candide drags a gentle young man across a world of war, earthquake, slavery, and ridiculous philosophy, always in the company of a tutor who insists that all is for the best. Voltaire's joke is relentless and exact.

Pangloss explains every horror as necessary. Candide's education is the slow failure of that explanation.

The famous close turns from cosmic theory to a small, workable task. The book earns that turn by refusing every easier comfort.

**Title:** Flagranti and other hilarities

**Themes:** Comedy, Manners, Surprise

## Synopsis: Flagranti and other hilarities

Flagranti and other hilarities offers being caught in the act, and other hilarities. The joke-word is the theme: surprise, embarrassment, the comedy of a door opened at the wrong second.

No author is named, so no particular farce will be summarized. The title is a promise of laughter at exposure.

These are light pages, by intention. The word hilarities is doing publicity and it is also doing genre.

**Title:** Rainbow

**Themes:** Weather, Sign, Hope

## Synopsis: Rainbow

Rainbow is a rainbow, and no author tells us whether the bow is weather, a promise, or a child's name for wonder. The image is a bridge of color after trouble, which is as far as the word itself will go.

A plot will not be hung on it. Rainbows in titles are signs: something has ended, or something is being offered.

The pages decide the theology or the landscape. The title only lifts the eye.

**Title:** The Hall farmer

**Themes:** Farm, Estate, Work

## Synopsis: The Hall farmer

The Hall farmer is the hall farmer, a cultivator tied to a great house or its land. The relation between the person who works and the hall that owns the view is the tension in the name.

No author, no family saga sketched beyond that. Rural labor and a big house are the setting the title consents to.

It belongs with stories of land, tenancy, and the dignity or strain of the work.

**Date:** 2022-09-18

**Title:** The second life

**Themes:** Renewal, Identity, Chance

## Synopsis: The second life

The second life promises a second life, the fantasy and the labor of beginning again. No author tells us whether the chance is spiritual, criminal, romantic, or simply a move to another town.

The theme is renewal after a first life has been used up. That is as far as a responsible summary goes.

Readers who have wanted a clean start will recognize the wish. The book decides what it costs.
