# 2022-09-21 — Selected fairy tales and To be or not to be

## Selected fairy tales

**Date:** 2022-09-21

**Title:** Selected fairy tales

**Author:** Hans Christian Andersen

**Themes:** Fairy Tale, Selection, Wonder

## Synopsis: Selected fairy tales — Hans Christian Andersen

Selected fairy tales is a selection of fairy tales, a chosen set rather than a single narrative. No author tells us whose wonders these are, so no particular glass slipper or wolf will be assigned.

The genre's promises still hold: transformation, a test, a justice that ordinary life postpones. Selection means a hand has chosen.

It is a book for reading one tale and then another, aloud if someone is listening.

## To be or not to be

**Date:** 2022-09-21

**Title:** To be or not to be

**Author:** Hans Christian Andersen

**Themes:** Faith, Doubt, Existence

## Synopsis: To be or not to be — Hans Christian Andersen

To be or not to be borrows Hamlet's question for a book by Hans Christian Andersen, who also wrote serious prose about belief and unbelief, not only fairy tales. The title itself is an existential test.

Pinning a cast and a conversion onto it would outrun what this note can honestly claim. Doubt and the wish to exist well are the theme the words give.

Readers who know only the tales may be surprised by the gravity of the question he was willing to put on a cover.
