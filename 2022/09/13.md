## Synopsis: Ariane - A Russian girl — Claude Anet

Ariane - A Russian girl is Claude Anet's novel of Ariane, a young Russian woman whose life is caught between the old world and the century that broke it. Love is the plot's surface. Displacement is the condition.

Anet writes a modern heroine, sexually and morally awake, against a Europe rearranging itself. The book was widely read as a contemporary romance with a historical undertow.

It is a story of a girl who refuses to be only someone's memory of Russia.

## Synopsis: Intoxication of transformation — Stefan Zweig

Intoxication of transformation is the novel published in English as The Post-Office Girl and in German as the intoxication of transformation. A young woman is briefly lifted into a life of hotels and ease, then sent back, and the return poisons what she can bear.

Zweig studies resentment as a chemical result of a glimpse. Love and crime become imaginable in the same lowered room.

The book is harsher than his nostalgic essays. It knows what a short holiday of wealth can do.

**Author:** W.Y.エバンス

**Themes:** Death, Judgment, Passage

## Synopsis: 閻魔帳 / Das Tibetanishce Totenbuch — W.Y.エバンス

閻魔帳 / Das Tibetanishce Totenbuch joins a Japanese name for the ledger of the dead with a German subtitle pointing at the Tibetan Book of the Dead. The subject those traditions share is an accounting after life, and a passage in which the soul is shown what it has been.

This edition is not a claim that the two books are the same text. The title sets them side by side: judgment, record, and the hope of a right recognition.

Readers interested in funeral rites and visions of the afterlife will find the theme in the title itself. The wording on the page keeps the spellings of this edition.
