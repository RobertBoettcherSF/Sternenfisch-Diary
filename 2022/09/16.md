**Date:** 2022-09-16

**Title:** Adieu

**Author:** Honoré de Balzac

**Themes:** War, Memory, Loss

## Synopsis: Adieu — Honoré de Balzac

Adieu is Balzac's story of a woman shattered by the retreat from Russia, and of the man who loves what remains of her. A single word she can still say carries the whole past.

The opening hunt and the later experiment in reconstruction are two ways of refusing to accept a ruined mind. Balzac is pitiless about the attempt.

It is a short, cold tale about love that wants the past restored on command.

**Title:** Physiology of Everyday Life

**Author:** Honoré de Balzac

**Themes:** Manners, City, Observation

## Synopsis: Physiology of Everyday Life — Honoré de Balzac

Physiology of Everyday Life sits in the nineteenth-century taste for 'physiologies,' little studies of how ordinary life behaves. With Balzac's name on it, the promise is social observation: types, habits, and the comedy of daily want.

I will not invent a cast of sketches the title does not list. What the book offers a browser is Balzac's attention turned on the street-level day rather than on a single dynasty.

It belongs with his curiosity about how people spend, pose, and get through an ordinary afternoon.

**Title:** Stories from the mountains

**Author:** Arthur Achleitner

**Themes:** Mountains, Tales, Place

## Synopsis: Stories from the mountains — Arthur Achleitner

Stories from the mountains is stories from the mountains, a regional gathering. Altitude, villages, weather, and the kinds of tale a valley tells are the materials the title itself stacks.

No author, no single plot. A collection is what the plural announces.

It suits a reader who likes place as a character and stories short enough to finish at a sitting.

**Author:** Unknown

**Themes:** Money, Power, Character

## Synopsis: The financier — Unknown

The financier names the financier, a person whose power is capital. No author is attached. Ambition, credit, and the moral weather around wealth are the themes the word reliably carries.

A particular bankruptcy or triumph will not be invented. The type is the subject.

Readers of social fiction know this figure. The book has to show which one.
