## Synopsis: The Hall farmer — Unknown

The Hall farmer is the hall farmer, a cultivator tied to a great house or its land. The relation between the person who works and the hall that owns the view is the tension in the name.

No author, no family saga sketched beyond that. Rural labor and a big house are the setting the title consents to.

It belongs with stories of land, tenancy, and the dignity or strain of the work.
