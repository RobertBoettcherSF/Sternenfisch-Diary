## Synopsis: Hoppelpoppel - where are you?

Hoppelpoppel - where are you? calls for Hoppelpoppel and asks where he is. A name like that, and a question like that, belong to a children's search: someone small is missing and the book is the finding.

No author is named. No woodland map will be drawn here. The emotion is the one in the question, a call sent after a beloved nuisance.

It reads as a book to be said aloud, because the name is already a pleasure to say.
