## Carta al Padre (Letter to Father)

**Date:** 2022-04-21

**Title:** Carta al Padre (Letter to Father)

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Language:** Spanish

**Themes:** Father, Son, Judgment

## Synopsis: Carta al Padre (Letter to Father) — Franz Kafka

Carta al Padre is Kafka's letter to his father, in the language of this edition. A son tries to explain a life that organized itself around a father's overwhelming presence.

Fear, the body, marriage, and writing are all brought into the explanation. The letter is a literary work because ordinary speech had no room for it.

It is painful and exact, a family argument raised to the level of form.

## Gripsholm castle

**Date:** 2022-04-21

**Title:** Gripsholm castle

**Author:** Kurt Tucholsky

**Themes:** Summer, Holiday, Love

## Synopsis: Gripsholm castle — Kurt Tucholsky

Gripsholm castle is Tucholsky's summer story of a holiday at Gripsholm, a playful escape shared by lovers and a friend. The castle is a toy setting for a few weeks of chosen leisure.

The book knows that a holiday is a fiction people agree to inhabit. It enjoys the fiction and notices the edges.

Reading it is like being allowed into a private joke that remains kind.

## Impaciencia del corazón (Impatience of the Heart)

**Date:** 2022-04-21

**Title:** Impaciencia del corazón (Impatience of the Heart)

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Language:** Spanish

**Themes:** Pity, Honor, Love

## Synopsis: Impaciencia del corazón (Impatience of the Heart) — Stefan Zweig

Impaciencia del corazón is the same Zweig novel under its other title, the impatience of the heart. A cavalry officer confuses compassion with love and learns the difference too late for the person he meant to help.

Austrian military manners and a provincial household make the cage. The psychology is the lock.

Zweig asks the reader to distrust a tenderness that needs an audience.

## Impatience of the heart

**Date:** 2022-04-21

**Title:** Impatience of the heart

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Pity, Duty, Shame

## Synopsis: Impatience of the heart — Stefan Zweig

Impatience of the heart is the novel Zweig also called a warning about pity. A young Austrian officer shows kindness to a disabled girl and then cannot tell help from love, or escape the role her family builds around him.

Good intentions tighten. The book is severe about sentiment that enjoys its own virtue and will not pay the ordinary price of honesty.

It ends as a tragedy of someone who wanted to be gentle and became cruel by refusing to stop.

## Letter to Father

**Date:** 2022-04-21

**Title:** Letter to Father

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Family, Authority, Writing

## Synopsis: Letter to Father — Franz Kafka

Letter to Father is Franz Kafka's long letter to his father, a reckoning he did not deliver as a conversation. He describes a childhood organized by a loud, successful man and a boy who experienced that strength as judgment.

Marriage, work, and writing all appear as attempts to answer a voice that was already in the room. The letter is precise about fear and also about the father's own history.

It is both a family document and a map of how authority gets inside a life.

## Little man

**Date:** 2022-04-21

**Title:** Little man

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Marriage, Unemployment, City

## Synopsis: Little man — Hans Fallada

Little man is Hans Fallada's novel of a young couple trying to stay decent in a city that will not employ them. A pregnancy, a thin wage, and the relatives who judge them turn a love story into an economic one.

Fallada's gift is the price of a sausage and the tone of a landlord. Politics sits in the kitchen.

The title's question, what now, is practical. The book refuses a glamorous answer.

## The invisible collection

**Date:** 2022-04-21

**Title:** The invisible collection

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Art, Collecting, Loss

## Synopsis: The invisible collection — Stefan Zweig

The invisible collection is Zweig's story of a collector who will not admit that his treasures are gone. During the inflation, a visitor is shown a room of master drawings that are no longer on the wall.

Pride, ruin, and the kindness of people who agree to see what is not there make the tale. It is short and sharp.

The subject is how a person protects a life by refusing the evidence.

## Young gentleman - very big

**Date:** 2022-04-21

**Title:** Young gentleman - very big

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Youth, Ambition, Size

## Synopsis: Young gentleman - very big — Hans Fallada

Young gentleman - very big uses a joking title, a young gentleman who is 'very big,' for a Hans Fallada story of youth and self-importance. The exact comic mechanism is the book's, not something to sketch from the adjective alone.

Expect a provincial or small-city scale and a hero whose estimate of himself is part of the problem. That is Fallada's home ground.

The title smiles. The prose, if it is like his other work, will not only smile.
