## Synopsis: The gambling den in Baden-Baden

The gambling den in Baden-Baden names the gambling hell of Baden-Baden and the card game Trente et Quarante. Edmond About aims at the casino as a social machine: elegance, ruin, and the comedy of people who think the table is a personality test.

The setting is the spa town where nineteenth-century Europe went to lose money politely. The game in the subtitle is not decoration. It is the ritual.

Expect satire of risk and respectability in the same room.
