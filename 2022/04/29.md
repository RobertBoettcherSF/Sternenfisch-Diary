**Author:** Leo Tolstoy

**Themes:** Folk Tale, Moral, Simplicity

## Synopsis: Folk Tales — Leo Tolstoy

Folk Tales belongs with Leo Tolstoy's plain tales for a wide readership, stories pared down toward a moral test. A peasant, a king, or a devil usually has to choose between a clever advantage and a costly right act.

The style is deliberately simple. Tolstoy wanted these to be told aloud, not admired as complexity.

They are short, stern, and often kinder than their severity first suggests.
