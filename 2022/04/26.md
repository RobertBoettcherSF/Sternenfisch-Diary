# 2022-04-26 — About writers and Aphorisms

**Date:** 2022-04-26

**Title:** About writers

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Writers, Essay, Reading

## Synopsis: About writers — Stefan Zweig

About writers is Stefan Zweig on writers, critical and biographical prose rather than his letters to them. He approaches an author as a fate that found a style.

The pieces are encounters, not a system of literary theory. Warmth and drama lead.

Readers who like his portraits of Balzac or Dickens will recognize the method, aimed here at a shelf of other lives.

**Date:** 2022-04-26

**Title:** Aphorisms

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Aphorism, Faith, Paradox

## Synopsis: Aphorisms — Franz Kafka

Aphorisms collects Kafka's short reflective sentences, including the late aphorisms in which belief, the Law, and the self argue in a few lines. They are not explanations of the stories.

Each one is a small trap. A comforting reading usually fails by the last clause.

The book is for readers who want Kafka's thought without a plot to hide inside.
