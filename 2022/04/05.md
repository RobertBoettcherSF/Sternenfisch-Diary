**Date:** 2022-04-05

**Title:** Among other things in the Pyrenees

**Author:** Kurt Tucholsky

**Themes:** Travel, Exile, Observation

## Synopsis: Among other things in the Pyrenees — Kurt Tucholsky

Among other things in the Pyrenees is Tucholsky writing from among other places, the Pyrenees included, in the years when Germany was ceasing to be a home. Travel notes, memory, and political weather share the pages.

The manner is still conversational. The lightness carries more freight than in Rheinsberg.

It is a book of a writer watching Europe from a temporary address.

**Date:** 2022-04-05

**Title:** Anton and Gerda

**Author:** Hans Fallada

**Themes:** Youth, Pair, Province

## Synopsis: Anton and Gerda — Hans Fallada

Anton and Gerda names Anton and Gerda, and it is one of Hans Fallada's early novels of a young pair in a provincial world. Public detail of the plot is thinner than for his famous later books, so this note will not invent episodes.

What can be said is the scale: two young people, a German town, and Fallada's habit of watching money and affection in the same scene.

Readers who know Little Man, What Now? will recognize the human size, in an earlier key.
