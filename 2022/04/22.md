# 2022-04-22 — Gripsholm castle and The castle in the moor

**Date:** 2022-04-22

**Title:** Gripsholm castle

**Author:** Kurt Tucholsky

**Themes:** Summer, Holiday, Love

## Synopsis: Gripsholm castle — Kurt Tucholsky

Gripsholm castle is Tucholsky's summer story of a holiday at Gripsholm, a playful escape shared by lovers and a friend. The castle is a toy setting for a few weeks of chosen leisure.

The book knows that a holiday is a fiction people agree to inhabit. It enjoys the fiction and notices the edges.

Reading it is like being allowed into a private joke that remains kind.

**Date:** 2022-04-22

**Title:** The castle in the moor

**Themes:** Castle, Moor, Place

## Synopsis: The castle in the moor

The castle in the moor sets a castle in the moor, a building in a wet, wide loneliness. No author is named, and no ghost will be installed in advance.

Place is the promise. Moorland and a great house are enough atmosphere for many kinds of tale, from family chronicle to night piece.

The book is about a site. What happens there is why a person turns the page.
