**Date:** 2022-04-10

**Title:** America

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** America, Youth, Exile

## Synopsis: America — Franz Kafka

America is Franz Kafka's unfinished novel of a European boy pushed out to America. Karl Rossmann arrives already in disgrace and moves through a country of hotels, elevators, and employers who rename him.

The book breaks off before a settled ending. What exists is a comic and anxious tour of work, patronage, and the feeling of being permanently new.

It is the lightest of Kafka's novels in scenery and still a story about not being let in.
