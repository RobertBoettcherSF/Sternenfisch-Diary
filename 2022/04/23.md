# 2022-04-23 — Favorite Castle, Jonathan Frock, and The Eight Octavo Booklets

**Date:** 2022-04-23

**Title:** Favorite Castle

**Author:** Luise Ahlborn

**Themes:** Home, Castle, Attachment

## Synopsis: Favorite Castle — Luise Ahlborn

Favorite Castle is Luise Ahlborn's Favorite Castle, a name that promises a place loved above other places: inheritance, refuge, or the romance of a house. Nineteenth-century domestic fiction often builds a plot out of exactly that attachment.

Inventing a siege, a ghost, or an heiress would be adding a different book. The castle as a beloved seat is what the title actually offers.

The pleasure advertised is place, and the feelings a place organizes.

**Title:** Jonathan Frock

**Author:** Heinrich Zschokke

**Themes:** Character, Dress, Name

## Synopsis: Jonathan Frock — Heinrich Zschokke

Jonathan Frock names Jonathan Frock, a person identified by a garment. A frock can mean a priest, a comic uncle, or simply a man whose coat is the joke. No author helps us choose.

The note stops at the name. A character book is what the title resembles, and the character is Jonathan.

Whoever he is on the page, the reader has already been introduced.

**Date:** 2022-04-23

**Title:** The Eight Octavo Booklets

**Author:** Franz Kafka

**Themes:** Notebooks, Writing, Thought

## Synopsis: The Eight Octavo Booklets — Franz Kafka

The Eight Octavo Booklets refers to the eight octavo notebooks Kafka kept, a mix of story openings, diary, and reflection. The pages show work in motion rather than a book he prepared for strangers.

A paragraph of fiction may break off into a private note. That discontinuity is part of what these notebooks are.

They are valuable if you want the hand behind the finished tales.
