## Synopsis: Fables — Abraham A Sancta Clara

Fables is a book of fables by Abraham a Sancta Clara, the baroque preacher famous for loud, comic, moralizing German. A fable here is a story with a finger in the reader's chest.

Individual tales will not be counterfeited in this note. The flavor is the author's: popular, excessive, and sure that vice has a ridiculous face.

It is devotional literature that grins.
