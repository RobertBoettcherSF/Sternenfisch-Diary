## Synopsis: La fin d'un monde (The End of a World) — Claude Anet

La fin d'un monde says an end of a world. Claude Anet often wrote about societies, especially Russian and European, as they ceased to be what their inhabitants had believed permanent.

The phrase is large. The honest summary stays large with it: a way of life reaching its last season, seen by a novelist of contemporary manners.

Detail of plot is the book's own. The title is already a verdict.
