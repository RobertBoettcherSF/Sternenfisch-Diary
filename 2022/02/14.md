## Synopsis: Letters to writers — Stefan Zweig

Letters to writers gathers Stefan Zweig's correspondence with other writers. The interest is the working life of letters: encouragement, disagreement, exile, and the practical business of books.

These are not a single narrative. They show a man who lived through other people's manuscripts as well as his own.

What a browser finds is the tone of a literary friendship, attentive and often urgent.

**Date:** 2022-02-14

**Title:** The love of Erika Ewald

**Author:** Stefan Zweig

**Themes:** Youth, Love, Music

## Synopsis: The love of Erika Ewald — Stefan Zweig

The love of Erika Ewald is an early Stefan Zweig novella about a young woman's first serious attachment, set near the world of music. Feeling outruns experience, which is the risk the story keeps circling.

It is slighter than his later novellas and already interested in the moment when emotion becomes a fate.

Readers who know Beware of Pity will recognize the younger version of that concern.
