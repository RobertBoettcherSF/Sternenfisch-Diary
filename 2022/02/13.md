**Themes:** Memory, Europe, Exile

## Synopsis: The world of yesterday: Memories of a European — Stefan Zweig

The world of yesterday: Memories of a European is Stefan Zweig's memoir of the European culture he grew up in and then watched come apart. Vienna before 1914, the habits of reading and travel, and the friendships of a literary life fill the early pages.

The later chapters follow war, inflation, the rise of dictatorship, and the loss of a passport that once opened borders. Zweig writes as a man already outside the world he is describing.

It is a book about a civilization remembered from exile, not a chronicle of battles. The tone is grateful and bereaved at once.
