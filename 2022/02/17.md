## Synopsis: Young gentleman - very big: Novel — Hans Fallada

Young gentleman - very big: Novel uses a joking title, a young gentleman who is 'very big,' for a Hans Fallada story of youth and self-importance. The exact comic mechanism is the book's, not something to sketch from the adjective alone.

Expect a provincial or small-city scale and a hero whose estimate of himself is part of the problem. That is Fallada's home ground.

The title smiles. The prose, if it is like his other work, will not only smile.
