## Synopsis: Bardo Thodol — Walter Evans-Wentz

Bardo Thodol belongs to the Tibetan Book of the Dead, a guide read for the dying and the dead through the interval between lives. The text describes visions, peaceful and wrathful, and repeats that they are projections of the mind.

The practical aim is recognition. If the dead person sees the appearances for what they are, release is possible; if not, another birth follows.

Editions and commentaries differ. The core remains a set of instructions for the hours when ordinary identity fails.
