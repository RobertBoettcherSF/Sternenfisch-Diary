## Synopsis: 道徳のマニュアル — エピクテット

道徳のマニュアル is the manual of Epictetus, a short Stoic training book. Distinguish what is up to you from what is not, and stop destroying yourself over the latter.

The chapters are blunt exercises in daily life. Slavery and philosophy meet in the author's own history.

It is a book to reread until a sentence becomes a habit.
