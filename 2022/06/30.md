## Synopsis: The Double — Fyodor Mikhailovich Dostoevsky

The Double is Dostoevsky's story of a minor official who meets another man with his face and his name, a double who is better at the life he is failing. Office politics turn into a pursuit through the streets.

The comedy is anxious. Golyadkin's attempts to explain himself only make him look more replaceable.

It is an early, nightmarish study of a self that cannot hold its place.
