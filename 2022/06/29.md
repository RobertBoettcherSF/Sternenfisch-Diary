# 2022-06-29 — God and the State, Heavy Blood, The Cossacks, and The Double

## God and the State

**Date:** 2022-06-29

**Title:** God and the State

**Author:** Mikhail Bakunin

**Themes:** Anarchism, Authority, Freedom

## Synopsis: God and the State — Mikhail Bakunin

God and the State is Mikhail Bakunin's attack on church and state as twin authorities over a free life. He argues that a divine master and a political one train the same habit of obedience.

The prose is polemical and unfinished in the way of a fighter's essay. Liberty, for him, is incompatible with a permanent ruler, sacred or secular.

It is a founding anarchist text, short and hot, not a constitution.

## Heavy Blood

**Date:** 2022-06-29

**Title:** Heavy Blood

**Author:** Juhani Aho

**Themes:** Temperament, Weight, Realism

## Synopsis: Heavy Blood — Juhani Aho

Heavy Blood is Juhani Aho's, and the title Heavy Blood announces temperament, inheritance, or a mood that will not lighten. Aho is a Finnish realist, exact about provincial feeling.

A plot summary firm enough to repeat is not in hand, so none will be invented. Blood in a title like this is usually character, not a crime scene.

The book asks to be read for its people and its climate, both inner and northern.

## The Cossacks

**Date:** 2022-06-29

**Title:** The Cossacks

**Author:** Leo Tolstoy

**Themes:** Caucasus, Youth, Freedom

## Synopsis: The Cossacks — Leo Tolstoy

The Cossacks sends a young Russian officer, Olenin, to a Cossack village in the Caucasus, where he hopes to escape a stale Moscow life. Hunting, a local woman, and the example of a fighter who does not need his ideas complicate the hope.

Tolstoy is already suspicious of a tourist's longing to become 'natural.' The landscape is precise and the romance does not flatter the newcomer.

It is a short novel about the fantasy of starting over among people who were already at home.

## The Double

**Date:** 2022-06-29

**Title:** The Double

**Author:** Fyodor Mikhailovich Dostoevsky

**Themes:** Identity, Clerk, Doppelgänger

## Synopsis: The Double — Fyodor Mikhailovich Dostoevsky

The Double is Dostoevsky's story of a minor official who meets another man with his face and his name, a double who is better at the life he is failing. Office politics turn into a pursuit through the streets.

The comedy is anxious. Golyadkin's attempts to explain himself only make him look more replaceable.

It is an early, nightmarish study of a self that cannot hold its place.
