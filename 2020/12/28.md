# 2020-12-28 — Carmentrans end and The tall Klas

**Date:** 2020-12-28

**Title:** Carmentrans end

**Author:** Paul Arène

**Themes:** Ending, Name, Mystery

## Synopsis: Carmentrans end — Paul Arène

Carmentrans end is opaque even as a phrase, something or someone called Carmentrans coming to an end. No author is named. Inventing a plot around that sound would be a fiction about a fiction.

What can be said is the structure of the title: an ending, and a name that does not explain itself. Endings are the oldest kind of story.

The book has to supply the rest. A summary that guessed it would be a different book.

**Date:** 2020-12-28

**Title:** The tall Klas

**Author:** Unknown

**Themes:** Stature, Character, Name

## Synopsis: The tall Klas — Unknown

The tall Klas names Klas and calls him tall. A physical fact becomes a character. In village tales, height is often a social fact too: someone seen above the others, for better or for mockery.

No author is given and no biography will be added. A man, a nickname from his body, and the life that nickname earns are the offer.

It sounds like a portrait novella. The pages have the rest of him.
