## Synopsis: Letters to Friedrich Hölderlin — Susette Gontard

Letters to Friedrich Hölderlin is Susette Gontard's letters to Friedrich Hölderlin, the poet who called her Diotima. They are the writing of a woman in a careful, constrained life, addressed to a love that could not be an ordinary household.

The historical frame is real: Frankfurt, a tutor in the house, a separation. The note will not invent the wording of particular letters.

What the book offers is that correspondence itself, a major document of Hölderlin's life and of hers.

**Title:** Pamphlets

**Author:** The White Rose

**Themes:** Conscience, Resistance, Leaflets

## Synopsis: Pamphlets — The White Rose

Pamphlets belongs to the White Rose, the student circle that wrote and scattered leaflets against the Nazi dictatorship. The texts call on readers to see the crimes of the regime and to refuse the comfort of not knowing.

They are not a systematic political theory. They are urgent pages, religious and humanist at once, written by people who expected to be caught.

Reading them now is reading a moral appeal made under a real risk, in plain printed sheets.
