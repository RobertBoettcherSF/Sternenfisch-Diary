**Date:** 2020-11-27

**Title:** The new teacher

**Themes:** Teaching, Arrival, School

## Synopsis: The new teacher

The new teacher is the new teacher, a classic situation: someone arrives in a school or a town and the place has to be learned from the front of the room. No author is named.

Authority, students, and the loneliness of the first term are the materials such a title almost always uses. Particular pupils will not be invented.

It is a vocation story. The newness is the plot's engine.
